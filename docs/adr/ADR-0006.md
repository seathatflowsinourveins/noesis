# ADR-0006: 6-Gate Statistical Falsification Across Independent Long & Short Legs

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Single-path backtests overfit parameters, conceal transaction-cost sensitivity, and hide asymmetric failure modes between long momentum entries and reversal-short legs.

## Decision Drivers

- Require strict pre-wall (`<= 2026-10-15`) multi-gate statistical falsification before Stage-5 live capital promotion.
- Correct for multiple testing and serial correlation strictly with OSI libraries containing `0` `yfinance` and `0` `quantstats`.
- Evaluate long and reversal-short legs independently rather than blending net returns.

## Considered Options

1. **Single train/test Sharpe threshold or legacy report tools pulling `yfinance`/`quantstats`** — fails under selection bias and violates the single-winner lockfile.
2. **6-Gate Statistical Falsification Pipeline (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`)** enforced independently across long and short legs with `MLflow` logging.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. Every candidate strategy clears all 6 upstream gates independently across long and short legs and logs artifacts to `MLflow`:
1. **`1x` and `2x` realistic transaction costs** (`BacktestEngine` fee/slippage stress).
2. **Rust `hftbacktest` L1/L2 queue-position & adverse-selection markout**.
3. **`skfolio` Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation (CPCV)** with `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`.
4. **`ml4t-diagnostic` Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) & Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO)**.
5. **`arch` Hansen Superior Predictive Ability (SPA)** test.
6. **`statsmodels` Newey-West HAC** autocorrelation-consistent t-statistics.

### Upstream Evidence & Official Directives

- **Falsification Stack:** `skfolio/skfolio`, `ml4t/diagnostic`, `bashtage/arch`, `statsmodels/statsmodels`, `nkaz001/hftbacktest`, `mlflow/mlflow`, plus vendored `ml4t-cpcv`, `ml4t-deflated-sharpe`, and `ml4t-backtest-tearsheet` skills.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Pull-in of `quantstats` transitively dragged `yfinance` into `uv.lock`, and hand-rolled Sharpe/bootstrap helpers risked statistical errors. Both were eliminated in favor of pinned upstream statistical libraries.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `uv.lock` zero `yfinance`/`quantstats` assertion (`tests/test_architecture_manifest.py`, `scripts/check.ps1`, `scripts/check.sh`), `deptry .`, and `tests/test_core_engine.py` (`test_six_gate_falsification_and_hftbacktest`).

### Compounding Layer Integration

- Powers the `sim-backtest` -> `live` promotion bridge: every falsification run writes structured 6-gate metrics and verdicts into `MLflow` (`:5000`), feeding continuous hypothesis refinement in `sim-backtest` and gating promotion dossiers in `live`.
