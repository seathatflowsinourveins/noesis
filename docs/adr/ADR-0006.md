# ADR-0006: 6-Gate Statistical Falsification Across Independent Long & Short Legs

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Single-path backtests overfit hyperparameters, hide transaction-cost fragility, and mask asymmetric failure modes between long momentum entries and reversal-short legs.

## Decision Drivers

- Pre-wall validation (`<= 2026-10-15`) before any Stage-5 live capital promotion.
- Multi-testing correction across trial counts and cross-validated paths using pure OSI libraries with zero banned `yfinance` dependencies.
- Independent falsification of both long and short legs rather than blended net metrics.

## Considered Options

1. **Single train/test Sharpe threshold or legacy report generators pulling `yfinance`** — fails under selection bias and violates the zero-banned-library lock.
2. **6-Gate Statistical Falsification Pipeline (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`)** enforced independently on long and short legs (`skfolio`, `ml4t-diagnostic`, `arch`, `statsmodels`, `hftbacktest`, `MLflow`).

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. Every candidate strategy must clear all 6 gates on both long and reversal-short legs independently and log artifacts to `MLflow`:
1. **`1x` and `2x` realistic transaction costs** (`BacktestEngine` fee/slippage stress).
2. **Rust `hftbacktest` queue-position & adverse-selection markout**.
3. **`skfolio` Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation (CPCV)** with `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`.
4. **`ml4t-diagnostic` Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) & Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO)**.
5. **`arch` Hansen Superior Predictive Ability (SPA)** test against null/benchmark.
6. **`statsmodels` Newey-West HAC** autocorrelation-consistent t-statistics.

### Consequences

- **Positive:** Blocks overfitted or cost-fragile strategies before live promotion with `0` `yfinance` transitive dependencies in `uv.lock`, while leaving `Sim` and `Paper` ungated for evidence collection.
- **Negative:** Requires sufficient historical sample density (`>= 16` CPCV splits) per leg.
