# ADR-0006: 6-Gate Statistical Falsification Across Independent Long & Short Legs

> [!NOTE]
> **Superseded by [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md):** This historical decision record has been consolidated into [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md) (*Institutional Backtesting, Microstructure Queue Simulation, 6-Gate Falsification & Sim==Paper==Live Trading Foundation*) to eliminate corpus duplication. All 6 statistical falsification gates (`MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`, independent `LONG` and `SHORT` leg evaluation) remain in full force under [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md).

## Context and Problem Statement

Single-path backtests overfit parameters, conceal transaction-cost sensitivity, and hide asymmetric failure modes between long momentum entries and reversal-short legs.

## Decision Drivers

- Require strict pre-wall (`<= 2026-10-15`) multi-gate statistical falsification before Stage-5 live capital promotion ([ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md)).
- Correct for multiple testing and serial correlation strictly with OSI libraries containing `0` `yfinance` and `0` `quantstats`.
- Evaluate `LONG` and reversal-`SHORT` legs independently rather than blending net returns, failing closed below `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16` (`DISCARD_INSUFFICIENT_TRADES`).

## Considered Options

1. **Single train/test Sharpe threshold or legacy report tools pulling `yfinance`/`quantstats`** — fails under selection bias and violates the single-winner lockfile.
2. **6-Gate Statistical Falsification Pipeline (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`)** enforced independently across `LONG` and `SHORT` legs with `MLflow` logging.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2, now consolidated and governed by [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md). Every candidate strategy clears all 6 upstream gates independently across `LONG` and `SHORT` legs via `evaluate_six_gate_falsification` and logs structured verdicts (`PROMOTE` / `DISCARD` / `DISCARD_INSUFFICIENT_TRADES`) to `MLflow`:

1. **`1_hftbacktest_l1_markout`:** Rust `hftbacktest` (`RiskAdverseQueueModel`) L1/L2 queue-position & adverse-selection markout (`passive_order_submitted == True`, `markout_bps >= -20.0`).
2. **`2_cost_1x_2x_survival`:** Positive total return and full falsification pass under `1x` (`4.0 bps`/side) and `2x` (`8.0 bps`/side) realistic transaction costs.
3. **`3_cpcv_oos_positive`:** `skfolio` `CombinatorialPurgedCV(n_folds=4, n_test_folds=2, purged_size=1, embargo_size=1)` with `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16` and positive OOS mean bps.
4. **`4_dsr_pbo_credible`:** `ml4t-diagnostic` Deflated Sharpe Ratio (`dsr >= 0.95` at true cumulative `n_trials_total`), Minimum Track Record Length (`n_trades >= min_trl`), and Probability of Backtest Overfitting (`pbo < 0.50`).
5. **`5_hansen_spa_significant`:** `arch` Hansen Superior Predictive Ability (`SPA`) test (`spa_p < 0.05` and `spa_p_upper < 0.05`).
6. **`6_hac_t_stat_significant`:** `statsmodels` Newey-West HAC autocorrelation-consistent t-statistic clearing the Harvey-Liu-Zhu multiple-testing threshold.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Blocks overfit, cost-fragile, or single-leg-masked strategies fail-closed (`DISCARD_INSUFFICIENT_TRADES` below `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`) using zero banned packages (`0` `yfinance`/`quantstats`).
- **Negative:** Requires at least `16` trades per independent `LONG`/`SHORT` leg; maintaining a duplicate falsification ADR alongside [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md) created drift risk resolved by formal supersession.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Single train/test Sharpe threshold + legacy report packages

- Good, because computation is trivial.
- Bad, because selection bias goes undetected and `quantstats` transitively drags banned `yfinance` into `uv.lock`.

### Option 2: 6-Gate independent-leg falsification pipeline

- Good, because queue markout, `1x`/`2x` cost survival, CPCV, DSR/PBO, Hansen SPA, and Newey-West HAC reject spurious strategies fail-closed across both legs.
- Bad, because sparse strategies below `16` trades per leg fail closed until sufficient pre-wall evidence accumulates.
