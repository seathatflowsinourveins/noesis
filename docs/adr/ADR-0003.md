# ADR-0003: Causal Point-in-Time Data Plane (`<= 2026-10-15`, `t_ns + 60s`, `XNYS`)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Backtests and signal pipelines fail silently when future information leaks through restated filings, bar-open timestamps, missing exchange-calendar half-days, or post-cutoff observations.

## Decision Drivers

- Enforce strict pre-wall PIT cutoff (`<= 2026-10-15` UTC, parameterized via `TIER0_PREWALL_CUTOFF`) for historical research.
- Enforce exact causal bar-availability lag (`ts_available_ns >= t_ns + 60_000_000_000`), SEC `accepted_ns <= as_of_ns`, and FINRA `T-1` publication alignment.
- Resolve US equity sessions (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`, `CLOSED`) and early-close days (`13:00 ET`) from one authoritative `XNYS` calendar helper (`engine.classify_et_hm` + `engine.classify_us_equity_session_et`).

## Considered Options

1. **Naive pandas resampling with weekday-only checks and unvalidated scraping** — leaks holidays, half-days, and lookahead bias.
2. **Causal `Polars` + `DuckDB` + `Pandera` + `Pydantic` v2 + `exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`) + `EdgarTools` + `FRED` PIT data plane**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 in `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py` and `src/tier0_sota/engine.py`. Historical pre-wall joins reject timestamps beyond `DISCOVERY_WALL_UTC_NS` (`PreWallViolationError`), validate bar schemas via `Pandera` and alert/manifest contracts via `Pydantic` v2, and classify 24/5 ET sessions strictly through `exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`) + `classify_et_hm`.

### Upstream Evidence & Official Directives

- **Data & Calendars:** `pola-rs/polars`, `duckdb/duckdb`, `unionai-oss/pandera`, `pydantic/pydantic` (v2), `stefanhoelzl/exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`), `dgunning/edgartools`, `mortada/fredapi`, `treeverse/dvc`.
- **PIT Discipline:** Official `ml4t-point-in-time` and `ml4t-polars-patterns` skills vendored in `.claude/skills/`.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Duplicating ET minute-of-day session math across `engine.py` and `tv/tickets.py`, or pulling unofficial scrapers (`yfinance`, `pandas-datareader`), caused edge-case bugs (e.g., `13:00 ET` early-close classification) and supply-chain leakage. Session math is now unified in `engine.classify_et_hm`.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-banned-library-import`, `no-yahoo-chart-api`), `uv.lock` `0` `yfinance` check, `tests/test_core_engine.py` (`test_causal_pit_regime_and_upstream_signals`, early-close assertions), and `tests/test_formal_and_evals.py` (`test_discovery_env_parameterization`).

### Compounding Layer Integration

- Provides verified causal PIT inputs to `sim-backtest` (`MLflow` trials) and `Dagster` asset `prewall_pit_audit` (`src/tier0_sota/definitions.py`), ensuring every downstream `paper`, `half-auto-tv`, and `live` compounding manifest builds on leak-free historical evidence.
