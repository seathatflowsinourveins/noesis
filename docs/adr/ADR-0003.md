# ADR-0003: Causal Point-in-Time Data Plane (`<= 2026-10-15`, `t_ns + 60s`, `XNYS`)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4
- **Sole Source of Truth:** SOTA upstream convergence per Core Trading Philosophy
- **Related:** [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md) (Multi-Tier Market, Microstructure, Borrow & Fundamental Data Feeds), [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) (24/5 Session Protection)

## Context and Problem Statement

Backtests and signal pipelines fail silently when future information leaks in. Common sources are restated filings, bar-open timestamps, exchange-calendar half-days the pipeline does not know about, short-interest settlement dates mistaken for publication dates, and observations after the cutoff. Feed selection and tiering (market tape, L2/L3 microstructure, borrow, fundamentals/macro) are governed by [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md). This ADR governs the single causal PIT contract that every tier must pass through.

## Decision Drivers

- Enforce a strict pre-wall PIT cutoff (`<= 2026-10-15` UTC, parameterized via `TIER0_PREWALL_CUTOFF`) for historical research.
- Enforce causal bar availability (`t_close_ns = t_ns + 60_000_000_000`), SEC `accepted_ns <= as_of_ns`, FINRA T-1 / publication-date alignment, and ALFRED `vintage_date <= as_of`.
- Resolve US equity sessions (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`, `CLOSED`) and early-close days (`13:00 ET`) from one authoritative `XNYS` calendar helper (`engine.classify_et_hm` + `engine.classify_us_equity_session_et`). The helper must stay consistent with the SIP 23x5 timetable effective 2026-12-06 (ADR-0013).

## Considered Options

1. **Naive pandas resampling with weekday-only checks and unvalidated scraping.** Leaks holidays, half-days and lookahead bias.
2. **Causal `Polars` + `DuckDB` + `Pandera` + `Pydantic` v2 + `exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`) + `EdgarTools` + `FRED`/ALFRED PIT data plane.**

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 in `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py` and `src/tier0_sota/engine.py`:

- Historical pre-wall joins reject timestamps beyond `DISCOVERY_WALL_UTC_NS` (`assert_pre_wall_date` / `assert_pre_wall_ns` raise `PreWallViolationError`).
- Bar schemas are validated via `pandera.polars` (`t_ns <= DISCOVERY_WALL_UTC_NS`). Alert and manifest contracts are validated via `Pydantic` v2.
- 24/5 ET sessions are classified strictly through `exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`) + `classify_et_hm`.
- The market data root defaults to `Z:\sota-data\prewall-lake` (override: `TIER0_MARKET_DATA_ROOT`).

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson learned:** two past mistakes caused edge-case bugs (for example, `13:00 ET` early-close classification) and supply-chain leakage:
  - duplicating ET minute-of-day session math across `engine.py` and `tv/tickets.py`;
  - pulling in unofficial scrapers (`yfinance`, `pandas-datareader`).

  Session math is now unified in `engine.classify_et_hm`. Calendar repository attribution was also corrected to the canonical `gerrymanoim/exchange_calendars` upstream.
- **Mechanical upstream gates:**
  - `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-banned-library-import`, `no-yahoo-chart-api`);
  - `upstream-map.toml` `banned_libraries` / `banned_packages`, and the `uv.lock` check for 0 `yfinance` entries;
  - `tests/test_core_engine.py` (`test_causal_pit_regime_and_upstream_signals`, early-close assertions);
  - `tests/test_formal_and_evals.py` (`test_discovery_env_parameterization`).

### Compounding Layer Integration

- Feeds verified causal PIT inputs to `sim-backtest` (`MLflow` trials) and to the `Dagster` asset `prewall_pit_audit` (`src/tier0_sota/definitions.py`). Every downstream `paper`, `half-auto-tv` and `live` compounding manifest therefore builds on leak-free historical evidence. The `live` lane stays at `live_execution_enabled=False` / `paper_only=True`.
- The feed tiers admitted by [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md) (Alpaca SIP/BOATS, Databento `MBP-10`/`MBO` via the NautilusTrader adapter once promoted, FINRA/Alpaca borrow, EdgarTools/ALFRED) must all pass through this PIT contract before reaching any lane.

  - the SIP 23x5 cutover (2026-12-06) requires session-classification changes;
  - a new feed tier is admitted under ADR-0013;

- Every amendment must re-measure the lake manifests and receipts listed above and re-verify `uv.lock` pins and SPDX licenses. It must also preserve `src/tier0_sota/risk.py` and fail-closed live-port isolation.
