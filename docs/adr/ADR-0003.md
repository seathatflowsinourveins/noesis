# ADR-0003: Causal Point-in-Time Data Plane (<= 2026-10-15, t_ns + 60s, XNYS)

## Context and Problem Statement

Backtests and signal pipelines fail silently when future information leaks in. Common sources are restated filings, bar-open timestamps, exchange-calendar half-days the pipeline does not know about, short-interest settlement dates mistaken for publication dates, and observations after the cutoff. Feed selection and tiering (market tape, L2/L3 microstructure, borrow, fundamentals/macro) are governed by [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md). This ADR governs the single causal PIT contract and canonical pre-wall lake receipt that every tier must pass through.

## Decision Drivers

- Enforce a strict pre-wall PIT cutoff (`<= 2026-10-15` UTC, parameterized via `TIER0_PREWALL_CUTOFF`) for historical research.
- Enforce causal bar availability (`t_close_ns = t_ns + 60_000_000_000`), SEC `accepted_ns <= as_of_ns`, FINRA T-1 / publication-date alignment, and ALFRED `vintage_date <= as_of`.
- Resolve US equity sessions (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`, `CLOSED`) and early-close days (`13:00 ET`) from one authoritative session-clock helper on the upstream `XNYS` (`exchange_calendars`) + `NYSE` (`pandas_market_calendars`) calendars (`engine.classify_et_hm` + `engine.classify_us_equity_session_et`). The helper must stay consistent with the SIP 23x5 timetable effective 2026-12-06 ([ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md)).
- Enforce **"LLM as Outer Research Operator Only"** (`Glasserman & Lin, arXiv:2309.17322`): because our historical pre-wall window (`<= 2026-10-15`) lies inside modern LLM training cutoffs, placing an LLM inside the per-bar or signal-generation path leaks future outcomes and introduces distraction bias; LLMs therefore operate strictly in the outer research loop (`MLflow`, `Optuna`, `Dagster`, `Inspect AI`, `DSPy/GEPA`, `nautilus-engine` MCP) while the inner signal/backtest loop remains 100% deterministic.

## Considered Options

1. **Naive pandas resampling with weekday-only checks and unvalidated scraping** — leaks holidays, half-days, and lookahead bias.
2. **Causal `Polars` + `DuckDB` + `Pandera` + `Pydantic` v2 + `exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`) + `pandas_market_calendars` (`NYSE` pre/post) + `EdgarTools` + `FRED`/ALFRED PIT data plane**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 in `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py` and `src/tier0_sota/engine.py`:

- Historical pre-wall joins reject timestamps beyond `DISCOVERY_WALL_UTC_NS` (`assert_pre_wall_date` / `assert_pre_wall_ns` raise `PreWallViolationError`).
- Bar schemas are validated via `pandera.polars` (`t_ns <= DISCOVERY_WALL_UTC_NS`). Alert and manifest contracts are validated via `Pydantic` v2.
- 24/5 ET sessions are classified strictly through upstream calendars + `classify_et_hm`: `exchange_calendars` (`XNYS`) decides session days and the regular open and close; `pandas_market_calendars` (`NYSE`) supplies the pre-market open and the post-market close (`17:00 ET` on a `13:00` early close). The overnight session (`20:00`-`04:00 ET`) has no upstream calendar and stays derived from the `XNYS` session list. A day whose upstream bounds cannot be used falls back to the pre-upstream bounds, and `engine.session_calendar_consistency(day)` reports why.
- The market data root defaults to `Z:\sota-data\prewall-lake` (override: `TIER0_MARKET_DATA_ROOT`).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Guarantees causal point-in-time isolation (`<= 2026-10-15`, `t_ns + 60s`, `XNYS` half-days) across every data tier and prevents lookahead leakage or banned scraper regressions.
- **Negative:** Rejects post-wall or unaligned timestamps fail-closed (`PreWallViolationError`), requiring explicit `TIER0_PREWALL_CUTOFF` advancement and manifest re-verification to extend the window.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Naive pandas resampling with weekday-only checks and scraping

- Good, because it requires minimal upfront schema setup.
- Bad, because it silently leaks half-day closes, restatements, and post-wall bars while violating the banned-library firewall (`yfinance`, `pandas-datareader`).

### Option 2: Causal `Polars` + `DuckDB` + `Pandera` + `Pydantic` v2 + `XNYS`/`NYSE` calendars PIT plane

- Good, because schema validation, causal timestamp lag (`t_ns + 60s`), and pre-wall cutoffs are enforced fail-closed in one shared module across all lanes.
- Bad, because extending the research window requires re-running pre-wall lake receipt checks and manifest verification.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson learned:** two past mistakes caused edge-case bugs (for example, `13:00 ET` early-close classification) and supply-chain leakage: duplicating ET minute-of-day session math across `engine.py` and `tv/tickets.py`, and pulling in unofficial scrapers (`yfinance`, `pandas-datareader`). Session math is now unified in `engine.classify_et_hm`, and calendar repository attribution is standardized on `gerrymanoim/exchange_calendars` (`XNYS` sessions, regular open and close) + `rsheftel/pandas_market_calendars` (`NYSE` pre/post bounds).
- **Mechanical upstream gates:** `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-banned-library-import`, `no-yahoo-chart-api`), `upstream-map.toml` `banned_libraries`/`banned_packages`, `uv.lock` check for 0 `yfinance` entries, `tests/test_core_engine.py` (`test_causal_pit_regime_and_upstream_signals`), and `tests/test_formal_and_evals.py` (`test_discovery_env_parameterization`).

### Compounding Layer Integration

- Feeds verified causal PIT inputs to `sim-backtest` (`MLflow` trials) and to the `Dagster` asset `prewall_pit_audit` (`src/tier0_sota/definitions.py`). Every downstream `paper`, `half-auto-tv`, and `live` compounding manifest builds on leak-free historical evidence while `live` stays `live_execution_enabled = False` / `paper_only = True` ([ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md)).
- The feed tiers admitted by [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md) (Alpaca SIP/BOATS, Databento `MBP-10`/`MBO` once promoted, FINRA/Alpaca borrow, EdgarTools/ALFRED) all pass through this PIT contract before reaching any lane.
