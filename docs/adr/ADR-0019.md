# ADR-0019: Execution Quality, Transaction Cost Analysis (TCA), Implementation Shortfall, Borrow-Fee Accounting & 24/5 Smart Order Routing (SOR)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4
- **Sole Source of Truth:** SOTA upstream convergence per Core Trading Philosophy

## Context and Problem Statement

The Core Trading Philosophy (enter extreme movers early, align with the causal regime, protect every order 24/5 with near-lossless `BE+fee` + 4-tier progressive trailing, flatten-first auto-reverse, `60s` rapid re-entry) only compounds if the cost assumptions inside the falsification gates ([ADR-0006](ADR-0006.md), [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)) hold on real fills. Today the repository *predicts* cost but does not *measure* it:

- **Predicted cost only.** `run_nautilus_signal_backtest` and `build_sandbox_live_node` (`src/tier0_sota/engine.py`) use `MakerTakerFeeModel(0.0004, 0.0004)`. Gate 2 assumes `4.0 bps`/side (`1x`) and `8.0 bps`/side (`2x`). `evaluate_hftbacktest_queue_markout` (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`) predicts passive `GTX` markout with `RiskAdverseQueueModel`, `FEED_LATENCY_NS = 50_000`, `constant_order_latency(100_000, 100_000)`, `taker_fee_rate = 0.0004`. No step checks these predictions against broker fills.
- **The paper ledger cannot support TCA.** `AlpacaPaperLane._record_order` (`src/tier0_sota/live/paper_farm.py`) writes `paper_wire_orders(ts_utc, account, lane_id, broker_order_id, symbol, side, qty, order_type, limit_price, stop_price, session, status, pnl_usd)`. It has no decision price, arrival quote, fill price, fill timestamp, fees, or borrow accrual. `pnl_usd` is written as `0.0`, and `counters["fills"]` is incremented at *submission*, not on broker fill confirmation. `PaperPerformanceAggregator` (`src/tier0_sota/live/performance.py`) therefore reports equity and realized PnL without a cost attribution.
- **Borrow cost is not modeled.** `select_short_or_inverse_fallback` verifies ETB (`easy_to_borrow is True`, `borrow_source == "alpaca_paper_asset"`) and falls back to `SOXS`/`SQQQ`. Neither path accrues a short-borrow fee, an HTB locate fee, or the holding drag of the inverse fallback. Paper accounts do not charge real borrow, so paper PnL overstates `SHORT`-leg edge.
- **Routing differs from broker rules across 24/5 sessions.** Brokers accept only limit orders outside RTH: Alpaca's 24/5 BOATS overnight session (20:00–04:00 ET) needs `LIMIT` + `extended_hours=True` + an `overnight_tradable` asset, and it rejects opening short sales. IBKR overnight needs `LMT` via `SMART` + `Order.includeOvernight` or the `OVERNIGHT` destination. Yet `SignalExecutionStrategy._submit_entry` builds `order_factory.market(...)` in every session, tagged `outsideRth`. `AlpacaPaperLane.submit_guarded_entry` sets `extended_hours` only for `PRE_MARKET`/`AFTER_HOURS` and attaches its `OTO` stop only in RTH. `IbkrPaperAdapter` builds `ProtectiveExitRateBudget` at `rate_budget_max_rpm=1000`, while the `IBKR_PAPER_4002` profile is `3,000 rpm`.

We need one upstream-native way to measure execution quality, decompose implementation shortfall, account for borrow, and pick routes per session and broker. It must feed the falsification gates and compounding manifests without inventing a TCA framework, without touching `src/tier0_sota/risk.py`, and with `live_execution_enabled = False` and `paper_only = True`.

## Decision Drivers

- **Close the predicted-vs-realized loop.** Each paper fill must reconcile against the cost the backtest assumed (`MakerTakerFeeModel`, `1x`/`2x` gate costs, `hftbacktest` markout). The reconciliation must be able to *falsify* a promoted strategy.
- **Standard decomposition, not invented metrics.** Use the Perold (1988) implementation shortfall: decision → arrival delay, arrival → fill execution/impact, explicit fees, borrow, and opportunity cost of unfilled quantity. Add arrival-price, interval-VWAP, and post-trade mid markout benchmarks in the style of amended SEC Rule 605 (compliance date `2026-08-01`: notional size buckets, odd lots, sub-second through multi-minute realized-spread horizons).
- **Native upstream data only.** Use NautilusTrader v2 execution events and reports, official `alpaca-py` trade updates and account activities, IBKR execution and commission reports through the paper gateway `:4002`, and SIP/BOATS quotes per [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md). Run joins as `DuckDB` `ASOF JOIN` / `Polars` `join_asof` with exact `Decimal` money.
- **Exits always win.** Routing must keep the `>= 10%` protective-exit RPM reserve (`ProtectiveExitRateBudget`), Rule-612 stop clamping (`<= 2.00%`), and the `-1.50%` latched fuse ([ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md)) in every session. Exits are never starved by entries, by other lanes, or by session transitions.
- **Borrow truth before shorting.** Accrue short-borrow cost with the industry convention (annual rate / 360, calendar-day accrual, Friday → 3 days, round-lot basis on Alpaca). Fail closed to the inverse-ETF fallback when the rate is unknown.
- **OSI-only licensing** per `spdx_license_firewall` in `upstream-map.toml`. No new runtime dependency is required.

## Considered Options

1. **Keep the static cost assumptions (`4.0`/`8.0 bps`, `MakerTakerFeeModel`) and treat paper PnL as ground truth.** Zero effort, but cost-model drift, adverse selection, and borrow drag stay invisible. Paper `SHORT` legs look better than they would live. Fills counted at submission inflate activity metrics. Rejected.
2. **Adopt a commercial TCA vendor or a third-party "TCA framework" package.** Institutional TCA suites are proprietary and not OSI-licensed. No maintained OSI Python TCA library exists in the 2026 ecosystem: the known open implementations are R-ecosystem `blotter` (`impShortfall`) or simulator slippage models such as `zipline`. Adopting one would add a second execution data model next to NautilusTrader and break `Sim == Paper == Live` parity. Rejected.
3. **Build a custom smart order router or venue-sweeping engine.** A retail-access workstation has no direct market access. Both brokers already run regulated, Reg-NMS-covered routers (IBKR `SMART`, Alpaca's routing including BOATS overnight). A self-built router is the self-invention that [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md) prohibits. Rejected.
4. **Upstream-native TCA and routing policy (chosen).** Measure execution quality from native NautilusTrader / `alpaca-py` / IBKR fill and fee events, joined causally to SIP/BOATS quotes in `DuckDB` + `Polars`. Decompose Perold implementation shortfall in exact `Decimal`. Reconcile against the `hftbacktest` + `MakerTakerFeeModel` predictions through the existing 6-gate thresholds. Express "SOR" as a declarative session × broker × side × borrow **route-selection policy** that only picks among broker-native routers, order types, collars, and TIFs, behind the immutable risk kernel.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 4. It extends [ADR-0004](ADR-0004.md) (parity), [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) (24/5 protection), [ADR-0008](ADR-0008.md) (market-aligned shorting), [ADR-0009](ADR-0009.md) (Paper Farm), and [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md) (borrow and quote feeds).

1. **Execution-quality records come from native upstream events only.**
   - **Sim / sandbox:** NautilusTrader v2 `OrderFilled` events (`last_px`, `last_qty`, `commission`, `liquidity_side`, `ts_event`) plus `engine.trader.generate_fills_report()`, `generate_positions_report()`, and `generate_account_report()`. Backtest venues keep the native `FeeModel` (`MakerTakerFeeModel`), `FillModel`, and `LatencyModel`. No shadow fill engine.
   - **Paper Alpaca (`ALPACA_PA1..PA3`):** the `alpaca-py` `TradingStream` `trade_updates` (`fill`/`partial_fill` events: `price`, `qty`, `timestamp`, `position_qty`), `TradingClient.get_orders`, and `get_account_activities` (`FILL`, `FEE`).
   - **Paper IBKR (`IBKR_DU_4002`, `:4002` only):** execution and commission reports through the NautilusTrader `InteractiveBrokers` adapter (`FillReport` / `OrderStatusReport`). IBKR generic tick `236` gives the shortable second opinion.
   - **Schema extension, additive only:** the paper ledger gains a broker-fill table keyed by `broker_order_id` with `decision_ts_ns`, `decision_mid`, `arrival_ts_ns`, `arrival_bid`/`arrival_ask`, `fill_ts_ns`, `fill_px`, `fill_qty`, `liquidity_side`, `commission`, `regulatory_fees`, `session`, and `route`. `counters["fills"]` comes from broker fill events instead of submissions. All money columns are `DECIMAL(18,6)` in DuckDB and `pl.Decimal` in Polars. Float is never used for cost attribution.
2. **Implementation shortfall uses the Perold decomposition with side sign `s = +1` (buy) / `-1` (sell); costs in bps of decision notional.**
   - `delay_bps = s · (arrival_mid − decision_mid) / decision_mid · 1e4`. The decision is the causal signal timestamp (`t_ns + 60s` bar availability, [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)).
   - `execution_bps = s · (VWAP_fill − arrival_mid) / decision_mid · 1e4`. This is effective-spread plus impact.
   - `explicit_bps = (commission + regulatory_fees) / decision_notional · 1e4`.
   - `borrow_bps`: accrued borrow over the holding period (item 4). `opportunity_bps = s · (horizon_mid − decision_mid) · unfilled_qty / decision_notional · 1e4` for expired, cancelled, or collar-rejected quantity.
   - Benchmarks per fill: arrival price, interval VWAP from decision to last fill (SIP/BOATS trades), and forward mid markouts at `1 s`, `5 s`, `60 s`, `300 s`. These bracket the amended Rule 605 realized-spread horizons, and `60 s` matches the causal bar latency.
   - Joins: `DuckDB` `ASOF JOIN quotes ON symbol AND ts >= quote_ts` (prevailing quote) plus forward `ASOF` at `fill_ts + horizon`, partitioned by symbol, with a staleness tolerance that drops fills lacking a fresh quote rather than imputing one. `Polars` `join_asof(by="symbol", strategy="backward", tolerance=...)` is the identical in-memory path. Results are reported per `account × lane_id × session × side × route` as notional-bucketed distributions (median, p90). A single mean is never reported on its own.
3. **Predicted-vs-realized reconciliation reuses existing gate thresholds; no new caps.**
   - Each roundtrip pairs its `hftbacktest` predicted markout and `MakerTakerFeeModel` fee with realized IS. Verdicts stay `DISCARD_INSUFFICIENT_TRADES` until `n >= MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES` (`16`) per leg, the same floor as [ADR-0006](ADR-0006.md).
   - Realized all-in per-side cost `<= 4.0 bps` (`1x`) keeps the backtest valid. A cost in `(4.0, 8.0]` bps makes the `2x` gate binding, and the strategy is re-falsified at measured cost. A cost `> 8.0 bps` (`2x`), or a realized 60 s markout `< -20.0 bps` (Gate-1 floor), falsifies the cost assumption: the lane is demoted to `sim-backtest`, and the 6 gates re-run with the empirically measured cost via `evaluate_six_gate_falsification(cost_bps_1x=..., cost_bps_2x=...)`.
   - `LONG` and `SHORT` legs are reconciled independently. `SHORT` includes borrow or inverse-ETF drag.
4. **Borrow-fee and short-financing accounting uses exact `Decimal`, shadow-accrued in paper.**
   - Direct short (ETB): `daily_fee = round_lot_up(qty) · prior_close · annual_rate / 360`, accrued every calendar day the position is held past settlement (Friday holdings accrue 3 days). Alpaca Trading API ETB rate is `0`. HTB adds the locate fee at locate time plus the daily rate. The IBKR collateral basis follows the broker's marked collateral value, and the rate comes from broker data (Tier C, [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md)).
   - Fail closed: if `easy_to_borrow` is not `True` from the broker asset API, or no HTB rate is known, `select_short_or_inverse_fallback` keeps routing to the `SQQQ`/`SOXS` inverse fallback. That fallback is charged its expense-ratio accrual and its daily-reset leverage drag (realized vs `-3x` underlying) as `borrow_bps` equivalents.
   - ETB → HTB transitions trigger the broker's pre-open cancellation of short orders. These are recorded as `opportunity_bps`, never silently dropped.
5. **24/5 route-selection policy (the "SOR") picks only among broker-native routers.**

   | Session (ET) | `IBKR_DU_4002` route | `ALPACA_PA1..PA3` route | Entry order | Protective exit | Opening shorts |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | `RTH` 09:30–16:00 | `SMART` | Alpaca default routing | Marketable `LIMIT` (collared) | Native `STOP_MARKET` (`OTO` on Alpaca) | ETB direct or inverse fallback |
   | `PRE_MARKET` / `AFTER_HOURS` | `SMART` + `outsideRth=True` | `extended_hours=True` | `LIMIT` only (`35 bps` collar) | IBKR native `STOP_LIMIT`; Alpaca via NautilusTrader `OrderEmulator`-triggered collared `LIMIT` | ETB direct or inverse fallback |
   | `OVERNIGHT` 20:00–04:00 | `SMART` + `includeOvernight` or `OVERNIGHT` destination, after `:4002` verification | BOATS via `extended_hours=True` + `overnight_tradable` (gated: `supports_extended_overnight_24x5 = False` on `ALPACA_PAPER_200`) | `LIMIT` only (`50 bps` collar) | Emulated collared `LIMIT` | Blocked on Alpaca (broker rule; already enforced) |

   - Collars and stop modes come from `SESSION_PROTECTION_24X5` and `compute_collared_limit_price`; no new parameters. `MARKET` entries outside `RTH` are non-compliant with both brokers. The engine's `_submit_entry` and the Paper Farm's `OVERNIGHT` `extended_hours` path are flagged for owner remediation under a separate engine change, and TCA records each broker reject as a `route_reject` event.
   - **Protective-exit rate fairness:** each account owns an independent `ProtectiveExitRateBudget`, and exits draw from the reserve first. Verified empirically: `IBKR_PAPER_4002` gives `max_rpm = 3000`, `exit_reserve_rpm = 300`, `active_entry_rpm = 2700`, and `ALPACA_PAPER_200` gives `max_rpm = 200`, `exit_reserve_rpm = 20`, `active_entry_rpm = 180`. `CrossLaneSymbolLock` prevents two lanes from competing for one symbol's exit. The `IbkrPaperAdapter` `1000 rpm` budget (reserve `100`) is recorded as profile drift to be aligned with `EXECUTION_VENUE_PROFILES`.
   - Live profiles (`IBKR_LIVE_3000`, `ALPACA_LIVE_PRO_LINE_1000`, `ALPACA_LIVE_ELITE_SMART_ROUTER`, which is `not_held`, `reg_nms_covered=False`) stay refused via `PermissionError`. Live ports `4001/7496/8798/8796` stay forbidden.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Closes the predicted-vs-realized cost loop in exact `Decimal` using native `NautilusTrader`/`alpaca-py`/IBKR fill events, `DuckDB`/`Polars` `ASOF` markouts, and broker-native 24/5 route policies.
- **Negative:** Requires additive broker-fill/IS schema updates in `paper_farm.py` and remediation of off-hours `MARKET` / `IbkrPaperAdapter` RPM drift before live cost reconciliation passes `n >= 16`.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson learned:** cost realism decayed silently because the repository only had *predicted* costs and a ledger that counted submissions as fills. Two tempting fixes are both self-invention and are prohibited: a home-grown "TCA engine" class, or a custom smart router that sweeps venues. TCA is declarative SQL/Polars over native upstream events. Routing is a policy table over broker-native routers. Neither may sit on the execution hot path or bypass `RiskEngineConfig(bypass=False)`.
- **Mechanical gates (existing, unchanged):**
  - `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-self-built-trading-engine-class`, `no-custom-shadow-engine-wrapper-class`, `no-risk-engine-bypass`, `no-hardcoded-live-execution-true`, `no-live-broker-endpoint`, `no-float-cast-in-risk`, `no-llm-on-execution-hot-path`, `no-banned-library-import`) via `ast-grep scan --config sgconfig.yml`.
  - `src/tier0_sota/risk.py` SHA-256 pin and `evals/formal_risk_z3.py` (`5/5` `UNSAT`).
  - `deptry .` and the lockfile assertions against banned libraries.
  - `gitleaks` (`.gitleaks.toml`) for paths and broker IDs.
- **Acceptance gates for the TCA implementation (to be added with it, not in this ADR):**
  - `pytest` property tests (`Hypothesis`) proving the IS components sum exactly in `Decimal` to `s · (VWAP_fill − decision_mid) · filled_qty + explicit + borrow + opportunity`.
  - A sign-convention test for `BUY`/`SELL`.
  - An `ASOF` staleness-drop test.
  - A borrow-accrual test covering Friday → 3 days and round-lot rounding.
  - A test asserting that no `MARKET` order is routed outside `RTH`.

### Compounding Layer Integration

- **`sim-backtest`:** each candidate stores its *predicted* per-side cost (`MakerTakerFeeModel` + `hftbacktest` markout) in `MLflow`. Re-falsification at measured cost (Decision Outcome item 3) logs a new run linked to the original `run_id`.
- **`paper`:** per-account DuckDB ledgers (`Z:\sota-data\manifests\paper_lane_*.duckdb`) gain the broker-fill and IS tables. `PaperPerformanceAggregator` extends `paper_farm_live_performance_manifest.json` and `finos_paper_farm_performance.json` with per-lane `median_is_bps`, `p90_is_bps`, `markout_60s_bps`, `borrow_bps`, `route_rejects`, and `fills_confirmed` (broker-confirmed). Panels are served through the official FINOS/OpenBB/Grafana stack ([ADR-0011](ADR-0011.md)).
- **`half-auto-tv`:** staged tickets (`semi_auto_confirmed = False`, `qty = 1`) carry the decision timestamp and decision mid, so manually confirmed fills are attributed with the same delay/execution split.
- **`live`:** Stage-5 promotion dossiers must include a reconciled TCA section per leg (`n >= 16`, realized cost within the `2x` assumption). Promotion stays blocked while `live_execution_enabled = False` and `paper_only = True`.
- **Cross-cutting:** verified cost lessons (for example, "overnight BOATS fills on low-float movers exceed `2x` cost") are deduplicated into `XERJ` (`:9200`). `scripts/sync_manifest.py --check` keeps `ARCHITECTURE_MANIFEST.json` drift-free.

### Living Evolution & Supersession Policy

- This ADR is living. Amend it in place when the following happen:
  - The amended Rule 605/606 data becomes ingestible as a broker-comparison prior.
  - The SIP 23x5 / BOATS timetable changes ([ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md)).
  - The NautilusTrader `InteractiveBrokers` adapter exposes `includeOvernight` natively.
  - Alpaca changes overnight short or order-type rules.
- **Earned-trigger upgrades:**
  - L2/L3 depth-aware impact attribution (Databento `MBP-10`/`MBO` through the native NautilusTrader adapter, per [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md)). Trigger: paper `execution_bps` exceeds the `1x` assumption on `>= 16` roundtrips in a lane where L1 cannot explain it.
  - Order-level impact stress through `microsoft/MarS` (`MIT`) ([ADR-0018](ADR-0018.md) frontier). Trigger: scaling notional beyond the paper sizing envelope.
- **Supersession:** a successor ADR must cite this ADR, keep the immutable risk kernel and the paper-only posture, re-run the reconciliation against at least the same `16`-roundtrip floor, and set this ADR's status to `Superseded by ADR-XXXX`.
