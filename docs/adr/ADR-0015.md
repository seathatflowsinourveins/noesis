# ADR-0015: Philosophy-Aligned SOTA Multi-Regime Strategy Suite: Extreme-Mover Longs, Causal Crash & Climax-Peak Shorts, Online Regime Detection & Portfolio Construction

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy asks for four things at once: find the highest-potential extreme movers early, align direction with the causal market regime (short market crashes and verified reversal peaks), protect every order 24/5, and compound only what survives falsification. [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) settles the order-protection layer and [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) settles whole-market scanning and ranking. Together with [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md), this ADR **supersedes [ADR-0008](ADR-0008.md) (Superseded)** and defines how strategies split into sleeves by regime, which upstream online-detection primitives may change the regime label, and how capital is shared across sleeves without hand-written weighting heuristics.

Implemented parts already in `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py` are `MarketRegimeActor` / `compute_causal_market_regime` (`+1` bull, `-1` crash, `0` neutral over `REGIME_BENCHMARK_SYMBOLS = ("SPY", "QQQ", "SOXX", "RSP", "VIX")`) and `generate_upstream_reversal_signals` using `ml4t.engineer` `market_regime_classifier`, `river.drift.ADWIN(delta=0.01)`, `ruptures.Pelt(model="rbf", min_size=5)`, and `stumpy.stumpi`. Execution and protection live in `SignalExecutionStrategy` (`src/tier0_sota/engine.py`), and falsification lives in `evaluate_six_gate_falsification` (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`, `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`, [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)).

## Decision Drivers

- **Philosophy fidelity:** every sleeve must map to one Core Trading Philosophy clause: extreme-mover longs, causal crash shorts (`regime == -1`), or climax-peak shorts after E2E-verified reversal.
- **Causality first:** regime and reversal labels must be computed online or point-in-time (`<= 2026-10-15` pre-wall, `t_ns + 60s` bar lag, `XNYS`, [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)). Offline segmentation (`ruptures`) is allowed only on the warm-up calibration window, never on future bars.
- **Upstream only:** detection, forecasting, conformal, causal, and portfolio primitives come from maintained OSI-licensed upstreams. No hand-written HRP, CVaR, conformal, or changepoint code.
- **Independent legs:** `LONG` and `SHORT` sleeves are falsified separately through all 6 gates ([ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)).
- **Immutable risk & fail-closed staging:** `src/tier0_sota/risk.py` (`SHA-256 ad8071dcd4196c4c529c02107114ebfdda6692bc9d7c220031f1b19ea3e4d847`, [ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md)) stays authoritative; staged semi-auto payloads strictly enforce `semi_auto_confirmed = False` and `qty = 1` ([ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md)); `live_execution_enabled = False` and `paper_only = True` stay in force until Stage-5 promotion ([ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md)).

## Considered Options

1. **Hand-tuned static regime rules plus equal-weight sleeves** — fragile at regime breaks and invites self-invented thresholds.
2. **Foundation-model-first direction (`Chronos-2` / `Toto` driving live long/short directly)** — strong zero-shot forecasters on GIFT-Eval, but neither is a causal regime classifier and neither has a 6-gate falsification record in this repo; kept shadow-only.
3. **Layered upstream multi-regime suite (chosen)** — online regime ensemble (`ml4t.engineer` + `river` ADWIN + `ruptures` Pelt warm-up + `stumpy` matrix profile) gating three regime-conditional sleeves over the shared [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) protection path and [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md) 6-gate falsification, with `skfolio` + active `Clarabel.rs` (`L5`) as the target cross-sleeve CVaR/CPCV allocator and `skchange`, `DoubleML`, `MAPIE`, `Chronos-2`, `Toto`, and `HiGHS` as earned-trigger shadow candidates.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 3 (superseding [ADR-0008](ADR-0008.md) (Superseded) alongside [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md)).

| Regime label (`MarketRegimeActor`) | Active sleeve(s) | Direction routing | Notional posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `+1` bull / FTD-confirmed | **Extreme-Mover Longs**: whole-market scanner ([ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md)) + Stockbee EP / momentum-burst / VCP setups | `LONG` only; climax-peak shorts only after `stumpy` + ADWIN reversal confirmation | Full kernel-sized (`risk.size_position_by_risk`) x session `max_notional_scale` |
| `0` neutral / transitional | Selective longs and confirmed climax-peak shorts | Both legs, each gated separately | Reduced by `skfolio` CVaR budget, never above kernel limits |
| `-1` crash / distribution cluster | **Causal Crash Shorts**: market-aligned shorts on regime flip, flatten-first auto-reverse from longs ([ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md)) | `DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` when verified ETB, otherwise fail-closed `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS_SQQQ` (`SOXS`, `SQQQ`, `SPXS`, `SDOW`); Alpaca overnight shorts refused | Kernel-sized; new longs blocked except inverse ETFs |

- **Climax-Peak Shorts** run in every regime only after a causal reversal: `stumpy.stumpi` left matrix-profile discord above the warm-up 75th percentile together with an `|r| >= ruptures`-calibrated threshold, or `river` ADWIN `drift_detected`. Intraday confirmation follows `step_rth_5m_bar` (`ORL5` break, first-red 5m, `VWAP` fail) with borrow / SSR / manual-confirmation gating (`semi_auto_confirmed = False` on every staged `TvAlertV1` payload; manual human confirmation occurs strictly outside the staged payload per [ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md) and `schema_v1.json`).
- **Portfolio construction (`L5` active stack; target `src/` wiring):** `skfolio` (`1.14.6`) + active conic solver `Clarabel.rs` (`clarabel==0.11.1`, `Apache-2.0`, graduated into `L5` active winners) provide `CombinatorialPurgedCV` (active in `research.py`) and target `HierarchicalRiskParity(risk_measure=RiskMeasure.CVAR)` / `NestedClustersOptimization` cross-sleeve allocation (target/research-only until multi-sleeve weights are wired in `src/`). Portfolio weights can only shrink `max_notional_pct` below kernel limits and never widen stops or bypass the `-1.50%` fuse.
- **Operator posture overlay (`half-auto-tv` / `paper`):** Vendored regime skills (`market-breadth-analyzer`, `uptrend-analyzer`, `market-top-detector`, `ibd-distribution-day-monitor`, `ftd-detector`, `macro-regime-detector`, `us-market-bubble-detector`, `exposure-coach`, `position-sizer`, `drawdown-circuit-breaker`, `pre-trade-discipline-gate`) remain advisory and journaled; they may block entries but never loosen `risk.py`.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Separates directional alpha into regime-gated sleeves backed by online upstream detectors (`ml4t.engineer`, `river`, `ruptures`, `stumpy`), active `L5` `skfolio` + `Clarabel.rs`, and strict `semi_auto_confirmed = False` staging beneath immutable `risk.py`.
- **Negative:** Cross-sleeve `skfolio` HRP/NCO portfolio weighting is currently target/research-only (not yet wired in `src/`), and newer adaptive upstreams (`skchange`, `DoubleML`, `MAPIE`, `Chronos-2`, `Toto`, `HiGHS`) stay shadow-only until clearing independent 6-gate falsification (`>= 16` CPCV samples).

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Hand-tuned static regime rules + equal-weight sleeves

- Good, because no statistical regime libraries are needed.
- Bad, because static rules break at structural volatility shifts and cannot be audited across regimes.

### Option 2: Foundation-model-first direction (`Chronos-2` / `Toto`)

- Good, because zero-shot multivariate forecasting captures cross-series patterns.
- Bad, because TSFMs lack causal regime semantics, add GPU latency, and have no 6-gate falsification proof in this repo.

### Option 3: Layered upstream online regime ensemble + regime-conditional sleeves + `skfolio`/`Clarabel.rs`

- Good, because each sleeve maps 1:1 to Core Trading Philosophy, fails closed under 6-gate falsification, and accepts shadow challengers cleanly.
- Bad, because promoting any shadow candidate or wiring multi-sleeve HRP/NCO requires dedicated CPCV runs and unit tests.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Earlier regime and allocation logic leaned on hand-picked thresholds and ad-hoc weighting; an earlier draft of this ADR also mistakenly wrote `semi_auto_confirmed=True` on climax-short staging, contradicting `schema_v1.json` (`const: false`). Regime labels now derive strictly from upstream online detectors, staged payloads enforce `semi_auto_confirmed=False`, and `Clarabel.rs` is reconciled cleanly as active `L5` rather than double-booked in `L13`.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `tests/test_core_engine.py`, `tests/test_live_automation.py`, `tests/test_tv_automation.py`, `evals/formal_risk_z3.py`, `evals/tier0_inspect_eval.py`, `deptry .`, SPDX OSI firewall, `ast-grep`, `ruff`, `ty`, and `just check`.
