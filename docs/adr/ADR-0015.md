# ADR-0015: Philosophy-Aligned SOTA Multi-Regime Strategy Suite: Extreme-Mover Longs, Causal Crash & Climax-Peak Shorts, Online Regime Detection & Portfolio Construction

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4
- **Sole Source of Truth:** SOTA upstream convergence per Core Trading Philosophy
- **Related:** [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md) (causal PIT), [ADR-0004](ADR-0004.md) (single-engine parity), [ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md) (immutable risk kernel), [ADR-0006](ADR-0006.md) (6-gate falsification), [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) (24/5 protection, trailing, reverse & re-entry), [ADR-0008](ADR-0008.md) (extreme-mover entry & regime-aligned shorting)

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy asks for four things at once: find the highest-potential extreme movers early, align direction with the causal market regime (short market crashes and verified reversal peaks), protect every order 24/5, and compound only what survives falsification. ADR-0007 and ADR-0008 settle the order-protection and scanner layers. They do not settle three things: how strategies are split into sleeves by regime, which upstream online-detection primitives may change the regime label, or how capital is shared across sleeves without hand-written weighting heuristics.

The parts already in the repo are `MarketRegimeActor` / `compute_causal_market_regime` (`+1` bull, `-1` crash, `0` neutral) and `generate_upstream_reversal_signals` in `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`, which use `ml4t.engineer` `market_regime_classifier`, `river.drift.ADWIN(delta=0.01)`, `ruptures.Pelt(model="rbf", min_size=5)`, and `stumpy.stumpi`. Execution and protection live in `SignalExecutionStrategy` (`src/tier0_sota/engine.py`), and falsification lives in `evaluate_six_gate_falsification` (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`, `MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`). Without one decision record, new sleeves, foundation-model forecasters, or optimizers could be added as custom shims, or used to override the immutable risk kernel.

## Decision Drivers

- **Philosophy fidelity:** every sleeve must map to one Core Trading Philosophy clause: extreme-mover longs, causal crash shorts (`regime == -1`), or climax-peak shorts after E2E-verified reversal. Any other sleeve is out of scope.
- **Causality first:** regime and reversal labels must be computed online or point-in-time (`<= 2026-10-15` pre-wall, `t_ns + 60s` bar lag, `XNYS`). Offline segmentation such as `ruptures` is allowed only on the warm-up calibration window, never on future bars.
- **Upstream only:** detection, forecasting, conformal, causal, and portfolio primitives come from maintained OSI-licensed upstreams with live PyPI/GitHub releases. No hand-written HRP, CVaR, conformal, or changepoint code.
- **Independent legs:** LONG and SHORT sleeves are falsified separately through the same 6 gates (ADR-0006). A sleeve that fails is discarded, not blended away.
- **Immutable risk:** `src/tier0_sota/risk.py` (SHA-256 `ad8071dcd4196c4c529c02107114ebfdda6692bc9d7c220031f1b19ea3e4d847`) stays authoritative: Rule-612 stop `<= 2.00%`, daily fuse `-1.50%`, protective-exit reserve `>= 10%`. Portfolio weights can only shrink notional below kernel limits, never raise it.
- **Fail-closed posture:** `live_execution_enabled=False` and `paper_only=True` stay in force until Stage-5 promotion (ADR-0002, ADR-0005).

## Considered Options

1. **Hand-tuned static regime rules plus equal-weight sleeves.** Simple, but fragile at regime breaks. It duplicates logic that upstreams already provide and invites self-invented thresholds.
2. **Foundation-model-first direction.** Chronos-2 or Toto forecasts would drive long/short directly. The models are strong zero-shot forecasters on GIFT-Eval, but they are not causal-regime classifiers, add GPU and latency costs, and have no falsification record in this repo. Rejecting them as the primary signal does not reject them as shadow candidates.
3. **Layered upstream multi-regime suite (chosen).** The online regime ensemble (`ml4t.engineer` + `river` ADWIN + `ruptures` Pelt calibration + `stumpy` matrix profile) gates three regime-conditional sleeves. Each sleeve runs through the shared `SignalExecutionStrategy` protection path and the 6-gate falsification. `skfolio` builds the cross-sleeve portfolio (HRP / NCO with CVaR, CPCV-validated). The newer upstreams (`skchange`, `DoubleML`, `MAPIE`, Chronos-2, Toto, `cvxpy` + `Clarabel` / `HiGHS`) are earned-trigger candidates evaluated in shadow mode first.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 3.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `+1` bull / FTD-confirmed | **Extreme-Mover Longs**: whole-market scanner (`min_rvol=1.5`, `LOW_MOVE_NOT_EXTREME` / `LOW_RVOL_NOT_EXTREME` excluded) plus Stockbee EP / momentum-burst / VCP style setups | LONG only. Climax-peak shorts only after `stumpy` + ADWIN reversal confirmation | Full kernel-sized (`risk.size_position_by_risk`) × session `max_notional_scale` |
| `0` neutral / transitional | Selective longs and confirmed climax-peak shorts | Both legs, each gated separately | Reduced by `skfolio` CVaR budget, never above kernel limits |
| `-1` crash / distribution cluster | **Causal Crash Shorts**: market-aligned shorts on regime flip, flatten-first auto-reverse from longs (ADR-0007) | `DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` when shortable and easy-to-borrow, otherwise fail-closed `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS_SQQQ` (`SOXS`, `SQQQ`, `SPXS`, `SDOW`). Alpaca overnight shorts refused | Kernel-sized. New longs blocked except inverse ETFs |

- **Climax-Peak Shorts** run in every regime but require a causal reversal: `stumpy.stumpi` left matrix-profile discord above the warm-up 75th percentile together with an `|r| >= ruptures`-calibrated threshold, or `river` ADWIN `drift_detected`. Entries go through `parabolic-short-trade-planner`-style ORL-break / first-red-5m / VWAP-fail triggers, with borrow / SSR / manual-confirmation gating (`semi_auto_confirmed=True`).
- **Portfolio construction:** sleeve return streams feed `skfolio` `HierarchicalRiskParity(risk_measure=RiskMeasure.CVAR)` or `NestedClustersOptimization`, validated with `skfolio.model_selection.CombinatorialPurgedCV`. The weights become the `max_notional_pct` scale passed to the kernel sizer and cannot widen stops or bypass the `-1.50%` fuse.
- **Operator posture overlay (half-auto-tv / paper):** the vendored regime skills `market-breadth-analyzer`, `uptrend-analyzer`, `market-top-detector`, `ibd-distribution-day-monitor`, `ftd-detector`, `macro-regime-detector`, `us-market-bubble-detector`, and `exposure-coach` produce the human-readable posture (new-entry-allowed vs cash-priority). `position-sizer`, `drawdown-circuit-breaker`, and `pre-trade-discipline-gate` enforce manual entry discipline. These skills are advisory and journaled. They can block entries but never loosen `risk.py`.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Earlier regime and allocation logic tended toward hand-picked thresholds and ad-hoc weighting. Neither can be falsified or audited across lanes. The regime label now comes only from upstream online detectors calibrated on the warm-up window. Cross-sleeve weights come only from `skfolio` estimators. Foundation-model forecasts stay shadow-only until a sleeve beats the incumbent through all 6 gates on both legs. Candidates enter `pyproject.toml` through a PEP 735 dependency group plus `uv.lock`, never through vendored copies or wrappers.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `tests/test_core_engine.py` (`test_causal_pit_regime_and_upstream_signals`, `test_nautilus_backtest_4tier_reentry_and_autoreverse`, `test_six_gate_falsification_and_hftbacktest`), `tests/test_live_automation.py` (`test_scanner_filters_flat_megacaps_and_enriches_trf_and_borrow`), `tests/test_tv_automation.py` (`test_tickets_strict_extreme_filter_rungs_and_short_routing`), `evals/formal_risk_z3.py` (Z3 UNSAT), `evals/tier0_inspect_eval.py` (`live_refusal`, `risk_sha256`, `zero_banned_libs`), `deptry .`, the SPDX OSI firewall in `uv.lock`, `ast-grep scan --config sgconfig.yml`, `ruff`, `ty`, and `just check`.

### Compounding Layer Integration

- **`sim-backtest`:** every sleeve × regime × trailing-schedule combination is an Optuna trial logged to MLflow (`tier0-sota-compounding-reflection`, `tier0-sota-falsification`). Sleeve verdicts, PBO, DSR, SPA, and HAC go to DuckDB under `Z:\sota-data\manifests` and to XERJ (`tier0-compounding-lessons`).
- **`paper`:** promoted sleeves dispatch through the scanner manifest (`live_whole_market_movers_manifest.json`) into the Paper Farm. Realized per-sleeve P&L feeds `paper_farm_live_performance_manifest.json`.
- **`half-auto-tv`:** regime-conditioned tickets with short routing (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` vs inverse hedge) are written to `live_half_auto_tickets.json`. The operator posture comes from the advisory regime skills.
- **`live`:** stays `stage5_promotion_readiness_gated`, with `live_execution_enabled=False` and `paper_only=True`. Promotion dossiers must include per-leg, per-sleeve 6-gate evidence plus Z3 and Inspect receipts.

### Living Evolution & Supersession Policy

- This ADR is living and evolvable, and it has no artificial ADR count cap. A candidate upstream (`skchange`, `DoubleML`, `MAPIE`, Chronos-2, Toto, `cvxpy` + `HiGHS`) is promoted to "Adopted" only after it (1) has a live OSI release, (2) beats the incumbent in a shadow MLflow comparison through all 6 gates on both LONG and SHORT legs with `>= 16` CPCV samples, and (3) passes `just check`. Promotion is recorded by amending this ADR in place.
- A material change to regime semantics (labels, feeds, sleeve set) or to the portfolio-construction estimator is recorded in a new ADR that marks this one `Superseded by ADR-XXXX`. Memory for superseded decisions is purged, and only philosophy-aligned SOTA memory is kept.
- No amendment can modify `src/tier0_sota/risk.py`, enable live execution, introduce banned libraries, or adopt non-OSI licenses.
