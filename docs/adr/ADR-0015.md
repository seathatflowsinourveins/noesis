# ADR-0015: Philosophy-Aligned SOTA Multi-Regime Strategy Suite: Extreme-Mover Longs, Causal Crash & Climax-Peak Shorts, Online Regime Detection & Portfolio Construction

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy asks for four things at once: find the highest-potential extreme movers early, align direction with the causal market regime (short market crashes and verified reversal peaks), protect every order 24/5, and compound only what survives falsification. [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) settles the order-protection layer and [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) settles whole-market scanning and ranking. Together with [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md), this ADR **supersedes [ADR-0008](ADR-0008.md) (Superseded)** and defines how strategies split into sleeves by regime, which upstream online-detection primitives update the regime label, and how capital is allocated across sleeves via wired `skfolio.optimization` + `Clarabel.rs` without hand-written weighting heuristics.

## Decision Drivers

- **Philosophy fidelity:** every sleeve maps to one Core Trading Philosophy clause: extreme-mover longs, causal crash shorts (`regime == -1`), or climax-peak shorts after E2E-verified causal reversal.
- **Causality first:** regime and reversal labels are computed online or point-in-time (`<= 2026-10-15` pre-wall, `t_ns + 60s` bar lag, `XNYS`, [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)). Offline segmentation (`ruptures`) runs strictly on the warm-up window.
- **Wired OSI upstreams only:** detection (`ml4t-engineer`, `river`, `ruptures`, `stumpy`) and portfolio construction (`skfolio.optimization` `MeanRisk`/`HierarchicalRiskParity` solved via `Clarabel.rs`) are directly wired in `src/tier0_sota/` with zero hand-written HRP/CVaR code.
- **Independent legs:** `LONG` and `SHORT` sleeves are falsified separately through all 6 gates ([ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)).
- **Immutable risk & fail-closed staging:** `src/tier0_sota/risk.py` ([ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md)) stays authoritative; staged semi-auto payloads strictly enforce `semi_auto_confirmed = False` and `qty = 1` ([ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md)); `live_execution_enabled = False` and `paper_only = True` stay in force until Stage-5 promotion ([ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md)).

## Considered Options

1. **Hand-tuned static regime rules plus equal-weight sleeves** — fragile at regime breaks and invites self-invented thresholds.
2. **Foundation-model-first direction (`Chronos-2` / `Toto` driving live long/short directly)** — strong zero-shot forecasters on GIFT-Eval, but neither is a causal regime classifier and neither has a 6-gate falsification record in this repo; kept shadow-only.
3. **Layered upstream multi-regime suite with wired `skfolio.optimization` + `Clarabel.rs` portfolio construction plus graduated `skchange` (`L2`) & `MAPIE` (`L5`) (chosen)** — online regime ensemble (`ml4t.engineer` + `river` ADWIN + `ruptures` Pelt warm-up + `skchange` anomaly segmentation + `stumpy` matrix profile) gating three regime-conditional sleeves over the shared [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md) protection path and [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md) 6-gate falsification (`8/3` CPCV `21` OOS paths `>= 16`, `cpcv_min_samples = 48`), with wired `compute_skfolio_portfolio_weights(...)` (`skfolio` + `Clarabel.rs`) and conformal trailing intervals (`MAPIE`, clamped `<= 2.00%`) in `src/tier0_sota/research.py`, leaving `DoubleML`, `Chronos-2`, `Toto`, and `HiGHS` as earned-trigger shadow candidates.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 3 (superseding [ADR-0008](ADR-0008.md) (Superseded) alongside [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md)).

| Regime label (`MarketRegimeActor`) | Active sleeve(s) | Direction routing | Notional posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `+1` bull / FTD-confirmed | **Extreme-Mover Longs**: whole-market scanner ([ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md)) + Stockbee EP / momentum-burst / VCP setups | `LONG` only; climax-peak shorts only after `stumpy` + ADWIN reversal confirmation | Full kernel-sized (`risk.size_position_by_risk`) x session `max_notional_scale` |
| `0` neutral / transitional | Selective longs and confirmed climax-peak shorts | Both legs, each gated separately | Reduced by `skfolio`+`Clarabel.rs` budget, never above kernel limits |
| `-1` crash / distribution cluster | **Causal Crash Shorts**: market-aligned shorts on regime flip, flatten-first auto-reverse from longs ([ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md)) | `DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` when verified ETB, otherwise fail-closed `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS_SQQQ` (`SOXS`, `SQQQ`, `SPXS`, `SDOW`); Alpaca overnight shorts refused | Kernel-sized; new longs blocked except inverse ETFs |

- **Climax-Peak Shorts** run in every regime only after a causal reversal (`stumpy.stumpi` left matrix-profile discord + `ruptures`-calibrated threshold or `river` ADWIN `drift_detected`) gated by `step_rth_5m_bar` (`ORL5` break, first-red 5m, `VWAP` fail), with borrow / SSR / manual-confirmation gating (`semi_auto_confirmed = False` on every staged `TvAlertV1` payload per [ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md) and `schema_v1.json`).
- **Wired Portfolio Construction (`src/tier0_sota/research.py`, `L5`):** `compute_skfolio_portfolio_weights(...)` invokes upstream `skfolio.optimization` (`MeanRisk` with `solver="CLARABEL"` and/or `HierarchicalRiskParity`, backed by graduated `clarabel==0.11.1`, `Apache-2.0`) returning validated non-negative weights summing to `1.0` with solver provenance `"clarabel"`, consumed directly in `scripts/run_compounding_reflection.py`. Weights can only shrink `max_notional_pct` below `risk.py` limits and never widen stops or bypass the `-1.50%` fuse.
- **Operator posture overlay (`half-auto-tv` / `paper`):** Vendored regime skills remain advisory and journaled; they may block entries but never loosen `risk.py`.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Separates directional alpha into regime-gated sleeves backed by online upstream detectors (`ml4t.engineer`, `river`, `ruptures`, `stumpy`), genuinely wired `skfolio.optimization` + `Clarabel.rs` portfolio allocation, and strict `semi_auto_confirmed = False` staging beneath immutable `risk.py`.
- **Negative:** Remaining shadow upstreams (`DoubleML`, `Chronos-2`, `Toto`, `HiGHS`) stay off live selection until clearing independent 6-gate falsification (`8/3` CPCV `>= 16` OOS paths, `cpcv_min_samples = 48` per leg).

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Hand-tuned static regime rules + equal-weight sleeves

- Good, because no statistical regime or conic solver libraries are needed.
- Bad, because static rules break at structural volatility shifts and cannot be audited across regimes.

### Option 2: Foundation-model-first direction (`Chronos-2` / `Toto`)

- Good, because zero-shot multivariate forecasting captures cross-series patterns.
- Bad, because TSFMs lack causal regime semantics, add GPU latency, and have no 6-gate falsification proof in this repo.

### Option 3: Layered upstream online regime ensemble + regime-conditional sleeves + wired `skfolio`/`Clarabel.rs`

- Good, because each sleeve maps 1:1 to Core Trading Philosophy, uses real conic optimization via `Clarabel.rs`, and fails closed under 6-gate falsification.
- Bad, because promoting any shadow candidate requires dedicated CPCV runs across both legs.
