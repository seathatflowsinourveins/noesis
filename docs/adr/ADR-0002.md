# ADR-0002: Four Dedicated Compounding Harness Lanes & Dual-Flywheel Learning (sim-backtest, paper, half-auto-tv, live)

## Context and Problem Statement

Quantitative engineering stalls when backtesting, paper trading, TradingView staging, and Stage-5 live readiness operate as isolated one-off scripts or passive status summaries instead of active compounding lanes connected to durable experiment ledgers, memory indices, scheduled orchestration, and empirical safety/reflection evaluations.

## Decision Drivers

- Operate 4 dedicated compounding harness lanes (`sim-backtest`, `paper`, `half-auto-tv`, `live`) anchored on one AAIF `AGENTS.md` SSOT (`CLAUDE.md` -> `@AGENTS.md`) and declarative `mise`/`just`/`prek` ([ADR-0012](ADR-0012.md)).
- Persist every pre-wall trial (`<= 2026-10-15`), paper fill/TCA receipt ([ADR-0019](ADR-0019.md)), and TradingView alert cycle into deterministic compounding manifests (`MLflow 3.18.0`, `Optuna 5.1.0`, `DuckDB 2.0.1`, `Z:\sota-data\manifests`, `XERJ`, `Caddy :8793`).
- Close a live, zero-shortcut Dual-Flywheel self-reflection loop linking empirical cross-leg quantitative falsification (`--mode lake|offline`, real `_compute_cpcv_pbo`, `skfolio`+`Clarabel.rs`, zero hardcoded `pbo=0.10`) with deterministic harness safety & quantitative prompt/ADR governance (`Inspect AI 0.3.292` 7/7, `DSPy 3.5.0` + `dspy.GEPA` via `OmniRoute :20128`, **8 active `Dagster 1.13.31` assets**, and `Orca` weekly Opus review), coordinating with [ADR-0018](ADR-0018.md), [ADR-0024](ADR-0024.md), and [ADR-0025](ADR-0025.md).
- Keep all LLMs, `dspy.GEPA`, and MCP tooling strictly off `src/tier0_sota/engine.py` and `src/tier0_sota/risk.py`.

## Considered Options

1. **Passive read-only lane reports, synthetic hardcoded PBO shortcuts, and ad-hoc markdown notes** — fails to close the empirical feedback loop across Sim, Paper, TV, and Live readiness, allowing strategy parameters and harness governance to drift.
2. **4 Active Compounding Harness Lanes + Saturated Dual-Flywheel Self-Reflection Layer (`NautilusTrader v2` + `hftbacktest` + `skfolio`/`Clarabel.rs` + `Optuna 5.1.0` + `MLflow 3.18.0` + `DuckDB` + `Inspect AI 0.3.292` 7/7 + `DSPy 3.5.0`/`dspy.GEPA` + 8 Active `Dagster 1.13.31` Assets + `XERJ` + `Caddy :8793` + `Orca` weekly Opus automation + `sync_manifest.py`) (chosen)**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt **Option 2** across four dedicated lanes and two continuously compounding flywheels:

1. **`sim-backtest` (`.claude/commands/lane-sim.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/sim.mcp.json`):** 24/7 `NautilusTrader v2` (`2.0.0rc9`) `BacktestEngine`/`Environment.SANDBOX` + Rust `hftbacktest` (`2.4.4`), strict causal PIT (`<= 2026-10-15`, [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)), upstream feature/regime/ML discovery (`LightGBM`/`CatBoost`/`SHAP`/`shapiq`/`skchange`/`sqlglot`) + `Optuna 5.1.0`, independent `LONG`/`SHORT` 6-gate falsification (`8/3` CPCV `56` splits / `21` OOS paths `>= 16`, `cpcv_min_samples = 48` with `DISCARD_INSUFFICIENT_PATHS`, Gate 1 `min_l1_fills = 16`, empirical `_compute_cpcv_pbo`, `skfolio`+`Clarabel.rs`+`MAPIE`, [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)) -> compounding `MLflow 3.18.0` manifests.
2. **`paper` (`.claude/commands/lane-paper.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/paper.mcp.json`):** Stationary Noesis 4-account Paper Farm (`IBKR_DU_4002` `:4002` `3,000 rpm` + 3 non-overlapping `ALPACA_PA1..PA3` `200 rpm` lanes via `CrossLaneSymbolLock`, [ADR-0009](ADR-0009.md)), real-time extreme-mover scanning & ETB/inverse routing ([ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md), [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md)), 24/5 collared `LIMIT`/trailing/auto-reverse/re-entry ([ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md), [ADR-0019](ADR-0019.md)), isolated `DuckDB` (`paper_tca_fills`) + `MLflow` fill/TCA manifests, and live `FINOS`/`OpenBB`/`Grafana`/`Prometheus`/`OTel` telemetry ([ADR-0017](ADR-0017.md), [ADR-0021](ADR-0021.md)).
3. **`half-auto-tv` (`.claude/commands/lane-tv.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/tv.mcp.json`):** TradingView Pine v6 cockpit, strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) bridge & staged semi-auto tickets (`semi_auto_confirmed = False`, `qty = 1`, [ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md)), closing telemetry back into Sim/Paper compounding manifests.
4. **`live` (`.claude/commands/lane-live.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/gate.mcp.json`):** Stage-1-to-Stage-5 promotion-readiness & `Sim == Paper == Live` `NautilusTrader v2` `LiveNode` parity lane governed by [ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md), `Z3` SMT proofs (`evals/formal_risk_z3.py`, [ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md)), `Inspect AI` 7-invariant safety suite, and cross-leg 6-gate + TCA promotion dossiers (live operations follow the three tiers of [ADR-0029](ADR-0029.md) on `ALPACA_LIVE_PRO_LINE_1000`: tier-1 telemetry, protective trailing and owner-lineage reverse/re-entry (lineage entries off today, [ADR-0029](ADR-0029.md) Build Status); tier-2 operator-confirmed tickets; tier-3 autonomous entries behind the two keys of the tier-3 gate (Key A: that leg's paper-merit record, with the Stage-5 6-gate dossier as one of its inputs; Key B: the owner's standing opt-in); `live_execution_enabled = False` means tier 3 is off).

   - **Flywheel A (Empirical Quantitative Falsification):** Supports `--mode lake|offline` (loading real pre-wall `Z:\sota-data\prewall-lake` Parquet slices `<= 2026-10-15` with explicit `data_source` provenance; `--mode offline` for deterministic CI).    - **Flywheel B (Harness Governance, `dspy.GEPA` & Active Dagster):** Runs `Inspect AI 0.3.292` (`evals/tier0_inspect_eval.py`, **7/7** deterministic invariants -> Inspect View `:7575`), `DSPy 3.5.0` + `dspy.GEPA` (`evals/dspy_self_reflect.py`: clean separation of offline wiring check vs `run_live_dspy_reflection`, quantitative `(score, feedback)` `philosophy_convergence_metric`, and `build_gepa_reflection_optimizer` targeting `OmniRoute :20128/v1`), **8 active `Dagster 1.13.31` assets** (`prewall_pit_audit`, `risk_kernel_sha256_attestation`, `paper_farm_performance_snapshot`, `inspect_safety_and_philosophy_eval`, `compounding_self_reflection_snapshot`, `adr_governance_and_graph_sync`, `sim_vs_paper_parity_reconciliation`, `frontier_trigger_telemetry` + `daily_compounding_reflection_schedule` `0 18 * * 1-5 America/New_York`), `XERJ` (`:9200`, `tier0-compounding-lessons` + `tier0-adrs`), `Caddy` (`:8793`), and `Orca` weekly Opus automation (`noesis-weekly-compounding-self-reflection`).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Connects pre-wall simulation, 4-account paper trading + TCA, TradingView staging, and Stage-5 readiness into one continuous empirical compounding OS with zero hardcoded PBO shortcuts, real `dspy.GEPA` prompt evolution, and 8 active Dagster assets.
- **Negative:** Adds operational surface across local portals (`:3000`, `:5000`, `:7575`, `:8080`, `:8793`, `:9200`, `:20128`) reconciled via `just resume` and `just reflect`.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Passive read-only lane reports + synthetic PBO shortcuts

- Good, because no local orchestration or experiment tracking portals are required.
- Bad, because hardcoded PBO and passive snapshots conceal real strategy decay, TCA drift, and harness regressions.

### Option 2: 4 Active Compounding Lanes + Saturated Dual-Flywheel OS

- Good, because every Sim, Paper, TV, and Live-readiness run writes verifiable cross-leg empirical receipts into `MLflow`, `Optuna`, `DuckDB`, `Dagster`, `Inspect View`, and `XERJ` while keeping LLMs strictly off the trading hot path.
- Bad, because operators must maintain the local 15-portal stack via `just resume` ([ADR-0017](ADR-0017.md)).

- **Lesson Learned:** Custom shell hook wrappers, hardcoded `pbo=0.10`, copy-paste `1.0` offline DSPy scores, and passive Dagster snapshot stubs undermined true compounding. All were replaced with empirical cross-leg falsification, live/offline-separated `dspy.GEPA`, and 8 active Dagster assets.
