# ADR-0002: Four Dedicated Compounding Harness Lanes & Dual-Flywheel Learning (sim-backtest, paper, half-auto-tv, live)

## Context and Problem Statement

Quantitative engineering stalls when backtesting, paper trading, TradingView staging, and Stage-5 live readiness operate as isolated one-off scripts or passive status summaries instead of active compounding lanes connected to durable experiment ledgers, memory indices, scheduled orchestration, and offline safety/reflection evaluations.

## Decision Drivers

- Operate 4 dedicated compounding harness lanes (`sim-backtest`, `paper`, `half-auto-tv`, `live`) anchored on one AAIF `AGENTS.md` SSOT (`CLAUDE.md` -> `@AGENTS.md`) and declarative `mise`/`just`/`prek` ([ADR-0012](ADR-0012.md)).
- Persist every pre-wall trial (`<= 2026-10-15`), paper fill/markout receipt, and TradingView alert cycle into deterministic compounding manifests (`MLflow 3.18.0`, `Optuna 5.1.0`, `DuckDB 2.0.1`, `Z:\sota-data\manifests`, `XERJ`, `Caddy :8793`).
- Close a live Dual-Flywheel self-reflection loop linking quantitative strategy falsification (Flywheel A) with deterministic harness safety & prompt/ADR governance (Flywheel B: `Inspect AI 0.3.292`, `DSPy 3.5.0` + `gepa` via `OmniRoute :20128`, `Dagster 1.13.31`, and `Orca` weekly Opus review), delegating multi-model auth & `L11..L15` frontier rules to [ADR-0018](ADR-0018.md) and executable ADR meta-governance to [ADR-0024](ADR-0024.md).
- Separate synchronous trading execution hot paths from asynchronous LLM/RAG/eval tooling (`Inspect AI`, `DSPy`, `XERJ`, `OmniRoute`).

## Considered Options

1. **Passive read-only lane reports and ad-hoc markdown notes** — fails to close the empirical feedback loop between Sim, Paper, TV, and Live readiness, allowing strategy parameters and harness governance to drift.
2. **4 Active Compounding Harness Lanes + Live Dual-Flywheel Self-Reflection Layer (`NautilusTrader v2` + `hftbacktest` + `Optuna 5.1.0` + `MLflow 3.18.0` + `DuckDB` + `Inspect AI 0.3.292` + `DSPy 3.5.0` + `Dagster 1.13.31` + `XERJ` + `Caddy :8793` + `Orca` weekly Opus automation + `sync_manifest.py`)**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt **Option 2** across four dedicated lanes and two continuously compounding flywheels:

1. **`sim-backtest` (`.claude/commands/lane-sim.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/sim.mcp.json`):** 24/7 `NautilusTrader v2` (`2.0.0rc9`) `BacktestEngine`/`Environment.SANDBOX` + Rust `hftbacktest` (`2.4.4`), strict causal PIT (`<= 2026-10-15`, [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)), upstream feature/regime/ML discovery + `Optuna 5.1.0`, independent long/short 6-gate falsification (`MIN_CPCV_SAMPLES = 16`, [ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)) -> compounding `MLflow 3.18.0` manifests.
2. **`paper` (`.claude/commands/lane-paper.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/paper.mcp.json`):** Stationary Noesis 4-account Paper Farm (`IBKR_DU_4002` `:4002` + 3 non-overlapping `ALPACA_PA1..PA3` `200 rpm` lanes via `CrossLaneSymbolLock`, [ADR-0009](ADR-0009.md)), real-time extreme-mover scanning & ETB/inverse routing ([ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md), [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md)), 24/5 trailing/auto-reverse/re-entry ([ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md)), isolated `DuckDB` + `MLflow` fill/markout manifests, and live `FINOS`/`OpenBB`/`Grafana`/`OTel` telemetry ([ADR-0017](ADR-0017.md), [ADR-0021](ADR-0021.md)).
3. **`half-auto-tv` (`.claude/commands/lane-tv.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/tv.mcp.json`):** TradingView Pine v6 cockpit, strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) bridge & staged semi-auto tickets (`semi_auto_confirmed = False`, `qty = 1`, [ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md)), closing telemetry back into Sim/Paper compounding manifests.
4. **`live` (`.claude/commands/lane-live.md`, `.claude/mcp-lanes/gate.mcp.json`):** Stage-1-to-Stage-5 promotion-readiness & `Sim == Paper == Live` `NautilusTrader v2` `LiveNode`/`InteractiveBrokers` parity lane governed by [ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md), `Z3` SMT proofs (`evals/formal_risk_z3.py`, [ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md)), `Inspect AI` 7-invariant safety suite, and cross-leg 6-gate promotion dossiers (`live_execution_enabled = False`, `paper_only = True` until a superseding Stage-5 ADR).
5. **Live Dual-Flywheel Self-Reflection Loop (`just reflect` -> `scripts/run_compounding_reflection.py`):**
   - **Flywheel A (Strategy & Quant Falsification):** Evaluates 3 deterministic pre-wall philosophy profiles (`core_24x5_compound`, `fast_reentry_cooldown_0`, `baseline_no_reverse`) over `NautilusTrader v2` + `hftbacktest` (`RiskAdverseQueueModel`) -> 6-gate statistical falsification -> `Optuna 5.1.0` study (`tier0_sota_trailing_robustness_v1`, `:8080`) and `MLflow 3.18.0` experiment (`tier0-sota-compounding-reflection`, `:5000`).
   - **Flywheel B (Harness Governance & Philosophy Alignment):** Runs `Inspect AI 0.3.292` (`evals/tier0_inspect_eval.py`, 7 deterministic invariants -> native `.eval` logs on Inspect View `:7575`), `DSPy 3.5.0` (`evals/dspy_self_reflect.py`: `CompoundingReflectionSignature`, `Tier0SelfReflectionProgram`, `philosophy_convergence_metric`, `build_omniroute_dspy_lm()` routed to `OmniRoute http://127.0.0.1:20128/v1`), `Dagster 1.13.31` (`src/tier0_sota/definitions.py`, 5 assets + `daily_compounding_reflection_schedule` `0 18 * * 1-5 America/New_York`, `:3000`), `XERJ` (`:9200`, `tier0-compounding-lessons`), `Caddy` (`:8793/compounding_self_reflection_manifest.json`), and `Orca` weekly Opus automation (`noesis-weekly-compounding-self-reflection`, `0 18 * * 5 America/New_York`, `claude --model opus --effort xhigh`).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Connects pre-wall simulation, 4-account paper trading, TradingView staging, and Stage-5 readiness into one continuous compounding loop (`MLflow`, `Optuna`, `DuckDB`, `Inspect AI`, `DSPy`, `Dagster`, `XERJ`, `Orca`).
- **Negative:** Adds operational surface across multiple local portals that must be reconciled via `just resume` and `just reflect`.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Passive read-only lane reports and ad-hoc markdown notes

- Good, because it avoids running local orchestration and experiment tracking portals.
- Bad, because strategy metrics, paper fills, and harness safety drift silently with no reproducible ledger or automated self-reflection.

### Option 2: 4 Active Compounding Harness Lanes + Live Dual-Flywheel Self-Reflection Layer

- Good, because every Sim, Paper, TV, and Live-readiness run writes verifiable manifests into `MLflow`, `Optuna`, `DuckDB`, `Inspect View`, and `XERJ`, while keeping LLMs strictly off the trading hot path.
- Bad, because operators must maintain the local 15-portal stack via `just resume` ([ADR-0017](ADR-0017.md)).

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Custom shell hook wrappers (`rtk`, `harness_directive_hook.py`, legacy `.codex` hooks), duplicated per-runtime instruction prose, ad-hoc manual reflection notes, and arbitrary command/agent line caps added brittle indirection without improving safety or strategy merit. All were purged in favor of upstream `Inspect AI`, `DSPy`, `Optuna`, `MLflow`, `Dagster`, `XERJ`, `Caddy`, and `Orca`.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `tests/test_architecture_manifest.py` (`zero_self_invented_hooks`, `CLAUDE.md == "@AGENTS.md"`, `5/5` native `uv` MCP parity, `46/46` lane & agent skill coverage), `tests/test_formal_and_evals.py` (Inspect invariants, DSPy reflection wiring, 5 Dagster assets & schedule, offline reflection CLI), `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-llm-on-execution-hot-path`), `just reflect`, and `just check`.
