# ADR-0009: Stationary 4-Account Paper Farm (`ALPACA_PA1..PA3` + `IBKR_DU_4002`)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Evaluating multiple intraday archetypes (`ORB Burst`, `Structural Trend`, `AVWAP Tight Lock`) simultaneously requires non-overlapping symbol ownership, isolated per-account telemetry, and strict separation from live real-capital endpoints.

## Decision Drivers

- Continuous ungated merit evidence across 4 stationary paper accounts on the Noesis host (`MAC_BROKER_PORTS = 0`).
- Zero symbol overlap across `ALPACA_PA1` (`L1_ORB_BURST`), `ALPACA_PA2` (`L2_STRUCTURAL_TREND`), and `ALPACA_PA3` (`L3_AVWAP_TIGHT_LOCK`), plus `IBKR_DU_4002` (`3,000 rpm`, 24/5 overnight).
- Unified live performance manifest feeding `FINOS/OpenBB` without custom web servers.

## Considered Options

1. **Single shared paper account with mixed strategies** — blurs attribution and causes cross-strategy order collisions.
2. **Isolated 4-account Paper Farm (`paper_only=True`, `live_execution_enabled=False`) with `CrossLaneSymbolLock` and isolated DuckDB stores**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 in `src/tier0_sota/live/paper_farm.py` and `src/tier0_sota/live/performance.py`. Each lane tracks independent status and `DuckDB` telemetry, acquires symbols exclusively through `CrossLaneSymbolLock`, enforces `ProtectiveExitRateBudget` (`200 rpm` Alpaca / `3,000 rpm` IBKR), and aggregates combined and per-lane equity, drawdown, fills, auto-shorts, reverses, re-entries, and fast-sells into `paper_farm_live_performance_manifest.json` + `finos_paper_farm_performance.json`. Live endpoints (`api.alpaca.markets`, `:4001/:7496/:8798/:8796`) remain hard-refused.

### Consequences

- **Positive:** Clean per-archetype live-market merit receipts with zero cross-lane interference or live-capital risk.
- **Negative:** Requires stationary Noesis credentials (`~/.config/tier0/.env.paper`) outside git for live paper submission.
