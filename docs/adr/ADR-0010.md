# ADR-0010: ToU-Compliant TradingView Pine v6 & Strict Pydantic v2 (TvAlertV1) Half-Auto Bridge

## Context and Problem Statement

TradingView powers the visual `half-auto-tv` compounding lane (Stage 3 under [ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md)), but browser automation, CDP debug-port attachment (`:9223`), repainting scripts, or hand-patched validators depending on undeclared transitive `jsonschema` (`DEP003`) violate TradingView ToU and upstream dependency hygiene.

## Decision Drivers

- Maintain 100% ToU-compliant display-only Pine v6 indicators (`barstate.isconfirmed`, `gaps=barmerge.gaps_off`, `lookahead=barmerge.lookahead_off`, zero CDP `:9223`).
- Validate every `tier0.tv-alert.v1` payload via strict upstream **Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`)** enforcing `extra="forbid"`, `qty=1`, `semi_auto_confirmed=false`, Rule-612 inward stops (`<= 2.00%`), `XNYS` session parity (`engine.classify_et_hm`), and borrow gates.
- Feed staged ticket and alert telemetry back into `sim-backtest` and `paper` compounding manifests.

## Considered Options

1. **CDP browser automation or hand-patched transitive `jsonschema` validation** — violates ToU and leaves `DEP003 = ["jsonschema"]` transitive debt in `pyproject.toml`.
2. **Display-only Pine v6 (`tier0_omni_sota_24x5.pine` + `tier0_market_regime.pine`) + strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) `tv/bridge.py` + Half-Auto `tv/tickets.py` + `scripts/tv_console.ps1`**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. Both Pine v6 indicators mirror the `3x4` trailing matrix, 4-tier progressive R-lock, `fast_sell_climax` auto-reverse, and `60s` re-entry ([ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md)) using valid Pine v6 colors and leading-zero JSON formatting (`tier0.tv-alert.v1`). `src/tier0_sota/tv/bridge.py` validates every alert through `TvAlertV1` (`pydantic==2.14.4`, aligned with `schema_v1.json`), `risk.py`, `XNYS`, and ETB borrow checks before atomic staging (`semi_auto_confirmed=false`, `qty=1`). `src/tier0_sota/tv/tickets.py` reuses `engine.classify_et_hm` and `TvAlertV1` to render order tickets and grouped TradingView watchlists.

### Confirmation

Confirmed mechanically via:

- All 7 tests in `tests/test_tv_automation.py` (Pine v6 syntax/non-repainting/R-lock parity, `TvAlertV1` session/Rule-612/borrow validation, atomic stage/ingest round-trip, strict extreme-mover tickets, PowerShell AST with zero CDP)
- `just deptry` (`0` issues, `0` `DEP003`)
- `just mcp-smoke` (`stage_semi_auto_order_preset`)
- `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-live-broker-endpoint`, `no-hardcoded-live-execution-true`)

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Delivers ToU-compliant, non-repainting TradingView Pine v6 cockpit visuals and strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) semi-auto alert validation with zero CDP `:9223` or transitive `DEP003` debt.
- **Negative:** Requires manual clipboard/webhook confirmation (staged tickets stay `qty = 1`, `semi_auto_confirmed = False` until the operator confirms through `just tv-confirm-paper`, or on the live lane, as tier 2 of [ADR-0029](ADR-0029.md), through the planned `just tv-confirm-live`, not yet a recipe) rather than headless browser automation.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: CDP browser automation + transitive `jsonschema` validation

- Good, because it could superficially mimic one-click TradingView clicks.
- Bad, because it violates vendor ToU, creates brittle UI breakage, and injects undeclared `DEP003` transitive dependency debt.

### Option 2: Display-only Pine v6 + strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) + zero-CDP `tv_console.ps1`

- Good, because every staged preset is type-checked in exact `Decimal`, stays 100% ToU-compliant, and shares `engine.classify_et_hm` and `risk.py` invariants.
- Bad, because human confirmation remains manual on every ticket (`semi_auto_confirmed = False`, `qty = 1`).

## More Information

### Upstream Evidence & Empirical Benchmarks

- **Validation & Charting:** `pydantic/pydantic` v2 (`2.14.4`, `MIT` — `BaseModel`, `ConfigDict(extra="forbid")`, `StrictInt`, `StrictBool`, `Field`, `field_validator`), TradingView Pine Script v6 specification, and `src/tier0_sota/tv/schema_v1.json` (JSON Schema Draft 2020-12 contract).

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Hand-patching transitive `jsonschema` required a `DEP003 = ["jsonschema"]` suppression in `pyproject.toml`, and duplicating ET session math in `tv/tickets.py` risked drift from `engine.py`. Migrating to declared upstream Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) and `engine.classify_et_hm` eliminated both completely.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `deptry .` (`0` issues, `0` `DEP003`), `tests/test_tv_automation.py` (7 tests), `verify_grand_reset.ps1` (`tradingview_pine_and_schema`), and `scripts/nautilus_mcp_server.py --smoke` (`stage_semi_auto_order_preset`).
