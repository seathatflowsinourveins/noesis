# ADR-0010: ToU-Compliant TradingView Pine v6 & Strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) Half-Auto Bridge

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4
- **Sole Source of Truth:** SOTA upstream convergence per Core Trading Philosophy

## Context and Problem Statement

TradingView powers the visual `half-auto-tv` compounding lane, but browser automation, CDP debug-port attachment (`:9223`), repainting scripts, or hand-patched validators depending on undeclared transitive `jsonschema` (`DEP003`) violate TradingView ToU and upstream dependency hygiene.

## Decision Drivers

- Maintain 100% ToU-compliant display-only Pine v6 indicators (`barstate.isconfirmed`, `gaps=barmerge.gaps_off`, `lookahead=barmerge.lookahead_off`, zero CDP `:9223`).
- Validate every `tier0.tv-alert.v1` payload via strict upstream **Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`)** enforcing `extra="forbid"`, `qty=1`, `semi_auto_confirmed=false`, Rule-612 inward stops (`<= 2.00%`), `XNYS` session parity (`engine.classify_et_hm`), and borrow gates.
- Feed staged ticket and alert telemetry back into `sim-backtest` and `paper` compounding manifests.

## Considered Options

1. **CDP browser automation or hand-patched transitive `jsonschema` validation** — violates ToU and leaves `DEP003 = ["jsonschema"]` transitive debt in `pyproject.toml`.
2. **Display-only Pine v6 (`tier0_omni_sota_24x5.pine` + `tier0_market_regime.pine`) + strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) `tv/bridge.py` + Half-Auto `tv/tickets.py` + `scripts/tv_console.ps1`**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. Both Pine v6 indicators mirror the `3x4` trailing matrix, 4-tier progressive R-lock, `fast_sell_climax` auto-reverse, and `60s` re-entry using valid Pine v6 colors and leading-zero JSON formatting (`tier0.tv-alert.v1`). `src/tier0_sota/tv/bridge.py` validates every alert through `TvAlertV1` (`pydantic>=2.10.0`, aligned with `schema_v1.json`), `risk.py`, `XNYS`, and ETB borrow checks before atomic staging (`semi_auto_confirmed=false`). `src/tier0_sota/tv/tickets.py` reuses `engine.classify_et_hm` and `TvAlertV1` to render order tickets and grouped TradingView watchlists.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Delivers ToU-compliant, non-repainting TradingView Pine v6 cockpit visuals and strict Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) semi-auto alert validation with zero CDP `:9223` or transitive `DEP003` debt.
- **Negative:** Requires manual clipboard/webhook confirmation (`qty = 1`, `semi_auto_confirmed = False`) rather than headless browser automation.

### Upstream Evidence & Official Directives

- **Validation & Charting:** `pydantic/pydantic` v2 (`BaseModel`, `ConfigDict(extra="forbid")`, `StrictInt`, `StrictBool`, `Field`, `field_validator`), TradingView Pine Script v6 specification, and `src/tier0_sota/tv/schema_v1.json` (JSON Schema Draft 2020-12 contract).

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Hand-patching transitive `jsonschema` required a `DEP003 = ["jsonschema"]` suppression in `pyproject.toml`, and duplicating ET session math in `tv/tickets.py` risked drift from `engine.py`. Migrating to declared upstream Pydantic v2 (`TvAlertV1`) and `engine.classify_et_hm` eliminated both completely.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `deptry .` (`0` issues, `0` `DEP003`), `tests/test_tv_automation.py` (Pine v6 balance/color/non-repainting, Pydantic v2 bridge, tickets, PowerShell AST), and `scripts/nautilus_mcp_server.py --smoke` (`stage_semi_auto_order_preset`).
