# ADR-0007: 24/5 Session Protection, 4-Tier Progressive Lossless Trailing, Climax Reverse & `60s` Re-Entry

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4
- **Sole Source of Truth:** SOTA upstream convergence per Core Trading Philosophy
- **Related:** [ADR-0004](ADR-0004.md) (single-engine parity), [ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md) (immutable risk kernel), [ADR-0006](ADR-0006.md) (6-gate falsification), [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md) (multi-regime strategy suite that consumes this protection layer)

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy requires protecting every order 24/5 (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`) with near-lossless breakeven+fee trailing, progressive profit locking, flatten-first auto-reverse, and rapid re-entry. Uncollared off-hours market stops risk severe slippage. Static trailing stops give back open R-multiples during climax reversals and miss continuation after stop-outs.

## Decision Drivers

- Unify ET session classification (`engine.classify_et_hm` + `engine.classify_us_equity_session_et`) and enforce `STOP_MARKET` in `RTH` vs collared `outside_rth` `STOP_LIMIT` off-hours.
- Implement monotonic `BE+fee` plus 4-tier progressive R-lock trailing (`+1R`, `+2R`, `+3R` / fast-sell), flatten-first climax auto-reverse, and `60s` rapid re-entry natively inside `NautilusTrader v2`, mirrored in Pine v6.
- Emit every trailing ratchet, auto-reverse, and re-entry transition into deterministic compounding manifests, verified end to end.

## Considered Options

1. **RTH-only static trailing stops with duplicated session logic.** Leaves extended and overnight positions unprotected and causes behavioral drift across lanes.
2. **Unified `3x4` `POOLED_TRAILING_SCHEDULES` + 4-Tier Progressive R-Lock + Climax Auto-Reverse + `60s` Cooldown**, realized inside `NautilusTrader v2` (`src/tier0_sota/engine.py`) and Pine v6.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2.

- **Schedule selection:** `NATR` picks one of three schedules: `tight_near_lossless` (`<= 1.5%`), `baseline` (`1.5%..3.0%`), or `wide_natr_adaptive` (`> 3.0%`).
- **Session stops (all 4 sessions):**
  - `RTH`: `1.50%` / `2.00%`.
  - `PRE_MARKET` / `AFTER_HOURS`: `1.25%` / `1.50%`, with a `35 bps` collar.
  - `OVERNIGHT`: `1.00%`, with a `50 bps` collar.
- **Progressive locks:** once `BE+fee` arms (`Tier 1`), the stop ratchets to:
  - `+0.45R` at `+1R` (`Tier 2`);
  - `+1.35R` at `+2R` (`Tier 3`);
  - `+2.35R` at `+3R` or climax exhaustion (`Tier 4`).
- **Reversal and re-entry:** a reversal flattens first, then enters the opposite leg. A same-side re-entry waits out a `60s` cooldown.

This layer is the shared protection path for every sleeve in [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md): extreme-mover longs, causal crash shorts, and climax-peak shorts.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Protects positions continuously across all 4 ET sessions (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`) with collared off-hours stops, monotonic `BE+fee` + 4-tier R-locks, climax auto-reverse, and `60s` re-entry.
- **Negative:** Tight off-hours collars (`35 bps` / `50 bps`) can leave `STOP_LIMIT` exits unfilled on extreme gaps, and `auto_reverse=True` paths bypass same-side re-entry cooldown sweeps unless tested separately.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson learned:** earlier LOC-golfing edits compressed `engine.py` and introduced a subtle `classify_et_hm` early-close defect when `rth_close_hm < 960`. All LOC-golfing was reverted. `classify_et_hm(hm, rth_close_hm=960)` is now clean and readable, is exported in `__all__`, and is shared by `engine.py` and `tv/tickets.py`. A second lesson from the reflection run: a parameter sweep is only informative if the code path it varies is actually exercised. Cooldown sweeps must therefore include sleeves with `auto_reverse=False` or same-side stop-outs.

### Living Evolution & Supersession Policy

- **Living ADR:** this ADR evolves in place as new reflection manifests land. The profile table is refreshed from the latest `compounding_self_reflection_manifest.json` rather than kept as a frozen snapshot.
- **What a parameter change needs:** a change to tier thresholds, lock floors, collars, or the `60s` cooldown needs an Optuna/MLflow comparison. The new value must beat the incumbent through all 6 gates (ADR-0006) on both LONG and SHORT legs, with `>= 16` CPCV samples, and must pass `just check`.
- **When a new ADR is required:** a structural replacement of the protection model (for example, a new trailing primitive or a different reverse semantics) is recorded in a new ADR that marks this one `Superseded by ADR-XXXX`, and superseded memory is purged.
- **Hard limits on any amendment:** no amendment can modify `src/tier0_sota/risk.py`, widen the `<= 2.00%` stop cap, or enable live execution (`live_execution_enabled=False`, `paper_only=True` until Stage-5).
