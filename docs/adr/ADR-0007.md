# ADR-0007: 24/5 Session Protection, 4-Tier Progressive Lossless Trailing, Climax Reverse & `60s` Re-Entry

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy requires protecting every order 24/5 (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`) with seamless near-lossless breakeven+fee trailing, progressive profit locking, flatten-first auto-reverse, and rapid re-entry. Uncollared off-hours market stops risk severe slippage, while static trailing stops surrender open R-multiples during climax reversals or miss continuation after stop-outs.

## Decision Drivers

- Unify ET session classification (`engine.classify_et_hm` + `engine.classify_us_equity_session_et`) and enforce `STOP_MARKET` in `RTH` vs collared `outside_rth` `STOP_LIMIT` off-hours.
- Implement monotonic `BE+fee` + 4-tier progressive R-lock trailing (`+1R`, `+2R`, `+3R/fast-sell`), flatten-first climax auto-reverse, and `60s` rapid re-entry natively inside `NautilusTrader v2` and mirrored in Pine v6.
- Emit every trailing ratchet, auto-reverse, and re-entry transition into deterministic compounding manifests verified end-to-end.

## Considered Options

1. **RTH-only static trailing stops with duplicated session logic** — leaves extended/overnight positions unprotected and causes behavioral drift across lanes.
2. **Unified `3x4` `POOLED_TRAILING_SCHEDULES` + 4-Tier Progressive R-Lock + Climax Auto-Reverse + `60s` Cooldown** realized inside `NautilusTrader v2` (`src/tier0_sota/engine.py`) and Pine v6.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. `NATR` selects among `tight_near_lossless` (`<= 1.5%`), `baseline` (`1.5%..3.0%`), and `wide_natr_adaptive` (`> 3.0%`) across all 4 sessions (`RTH` `1.50%`/`2.00%`, `PRE_MARKET`/`AFTER_HOURS` `1.25%`/`1.50%` with `35 bps` collar, `OVERNIGHT` `1.00%` with `50 bps` collar). Once `BE+fee` arms (`Tier 1`), progressive locks ratchet to `+0.45R` at `+1R` (`Tier 2`), `+1.35R` at `+2R` (`Tier 3`), and `+2.35R` at `+3R` or climax exhaustion (`Tier 4`). Reversals flatten first before entering the opposite leg, and re-entry enforces a `60s` cooldown.
