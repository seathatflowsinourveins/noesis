# ADR-0007: 24/5 Session Protection, 4-Tier Progressive Lossless Trailing, Climax Reverse & `60s` Re-Entry

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

US equities trade across distinct liquidity regimes (`PRE_MARKET`, `RTH`, `AFTER_HOURS`, `OVERNIGHT`). Uncollared `STOP_MARKET` orders risk catastrophic fills off-hours, while static trailing stops surrender open R-multiples during climax reversals or miss rapid continuation after stops.

## Decision Drivers

- Session-aware stop order modes (`STOP_MARKET` in `RTH`; collared `STOP_LIMIT` with `outside_rth=True` off-hours).
- Monotonic near-lossless trailing locking breakeven + fees (`BE+`) and progressive R-multiples (`+1R`, `+2R`, `+3R/fast-sell`).
- Flatten-first auto-reverse on climax exhaustion and rapid `60s` re-entry without orphan exposure.

## Considered Options

1. **RTH-only static trailing stops** — leaves overnight/extended positions unprotected and gives back parabolic gains.
2. **3x4 `POOLED_TRAILING_SCHEDULES` + 4-Tier Progressive R-Lock + Climax Auto-Reverse + `60s` Cooldown** across `engine.py` and Pine v6.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. `NATR` selects among `tight_near_lossless` (`<= 1.5%`), `baseline` (`1.5%..3.0%`), and `wide_natr_adaptive` (`> 3.0%`) across all 4 sessions (`RTH` `1.50%`/`2.00%`, `PRE_MARKET`/`AFTER_HOURS` `1.25%`/`1.50%` with `35 bps` collar, `OVERNIGHT` `1.00%` with `50 bps` collar). Once `BE+fee` arms (`Tier 1`), progressive R-locks ratchet to `+0.45R` at `+1R` (`Tier 2`), `+1.35R` at `+2R` (`Tier 3`), and `+2.35R` at `+3R` or parabolic climax (`Tier 4`). Reversals flatten first before entering the opposite leg, and re-entry enforces a `60s` minimum cooldown.

### Consequences

- **Positive:** 24/5 capital preservation with unified `NautilusTrader v2` and TradingView Pine v6 stop-rung parity.
- **Negative:** Collared `STOP_LIMIT` exits require sufficient collar width (`35`–`50 bps`) to remain fillable in thin books.
