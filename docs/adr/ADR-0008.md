# ADR-0008: Extreme-Mover Early Entry, Causal Regime Alignment & Market-Aligned Shorting

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4
- **Sole Source of Truth:** SOTA upstream convergence per Core Trading Philosophy

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy (`PHILOSOPHY_COMPOUNDING_MANIFEST.json`) focuses capital and broker API rate budgets strictly on the **highest-potential extreme movers early** while aligning long/short direction with causal multi-feed market regime (shorting market crashes where `regime == -1` and verified reversal peaks). Spending scarce API budget (`200 rpm` per Alpaca Paper lane across `ALPACA_PA1..PA3` / `3,000 rpm` on IBKR Paper `IBKR_DU_4002`, each reserving `>= 10%` capacity strictly for protective exits) on flat, low-RVOL mega-caps or fighting broad market selloffs degrades compounding expectancy and exposes accounts to unborrowable short rejections.

This decision establishes the core extreme-mover selection, causal regime alignment, and fail-closed short-routing contract across `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/scanner.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/paper_farm.py`, and `src/tier0_sota/tv/tickets.py`. Its deep-dive whole-market 24/5 scanning, PIT catalyst filtering, and cross-sectional Learning-to-Rank architecture is detailed in [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md), while its multi-regime strategy suite, online changepoint/regime detection, and portfolio construction are detailed in [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md) (building on [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md), [ADR-0007](ADR-0007.md), and [ADR-0013](ADR-0013.md)).

## Decision Drivers

- **Focus Strictly on Extreme Movers Early:** Reject flat/low-RVOL symbols (`LOW_MOVE_NOT_EXTREME`, `LOW_RVOL_NOT_EXTREME`) unless pinned (`SOXL`, `SOXS`) or acting as crash inverse hedges (`SOXS`, `SQQQ`) when `regime == -1`, applying quantitative thresholds (`|session_move_pct| >= 3.5%`, `rvol_normalized >= 1.5x`, `price >= $1.50`, `dollar_vol >= $5,000,000`).
- **Align Direction with Causal Multi-Feed Market Regime:** Compute causal `SPY`/`QQQ`/`SOXX`/`RSP`/`VIX` regime (`+1` bull, `-1` crash/bear, `0` neutral) strictly on closed pre-wall/live bars (`t_close_ns = t_ns + 60s`, `XNYS`), shorting market crashes (`regime == -1`) and entering extreme movers early via upstream feature, drift, changepoint, and matrix-profile libraries.
- **Short Reversal Peaks Only After Causal Confirmation:** Require E2E-verified causal reversal confirmation (`step_rth_5m_bar` RTH 5-minute `ORL5` break, first-red 5m, or `VWAP` fail in `discovery.py`, mirrored in `pine/tier0_omni_sota_24x5.pine` with `barstate.isconfirmed`).
- **Enforce Fail-Closed Short Borrow Verification & Session Protection:** Verify `shortable && easy_to_borrow` (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB`) post-selection via `alpaca_paper_asset` metadata, block overnight direct shorts on Alpaca, route unverified or hard-to-borrow shorts fail-closed to inverse ETF hedges (`SOXS`/`SQQQ`), and keep `live_execution_enabled = False` and `paper_only = True`.

## Considered Options

1. **Static large-cap watchlists with unconstrained long/short entries** — wastes RPM on low-movement symbols, fights broad crash selloffs, lacks peak-reversal confirmation, and risks HTB/unborrowable short rejections.
2. **Whole-market extreme-mover scanner (`alpaca-py` SIP + `FINRA TRF`) + upstream causal regime/reversal discovery + post-selection ETB lookup + fail-closed inverse ETF fallback**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 across `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/scanner.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/paper_farm.py`, and `src/tier0_sota/tv/tickets.py`:

- **Whole-Market Extreme-Mover Gate (`live/scanner.py`, [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md)):** `ExtremeMoverScanner` queries `ScreenerClient` (`get_most_actives(top=30, by=VOLUME)`, `get_market_movers(top=30)`), merges `DEFAULT_PIN_SYMBOLS = ("SOXL", "SOXS")`, enriches with `DataFeed.SIP` 1-minute bars (6h) and SIP trades (4h, FINRA TRF `Exchange.D` ratio via `aggregate_finra_trf_ratios`), filters out flat/sleepy names below `3.5%` move / `1.5x` RVOL / `$1.50` / `$5M` dollar volume, ranks deterministically (`abs(mv) * 2.0 + log1p(rvol) * 10.0 + trf * 8.0`, `top_k = 15`), and binds exact `Decimal` Rule-612 session stops (`<= 2.00%`).
- **Causal Multi-Feed Regime & Peak-Reversal Confirmation (`discovery.py`, [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md)):** `MarketRegimeActor` (`DataActor` native in `NautilusTrader v2`) and `compute_causal_market_regime` evaluate `SPY`/`QQQ`/`SOXX`/`VIX` (`trend_window=30`, `crash_ret_threshold=-0.0035`, `bull_ret_threshold=0.0020`, `vix_stress_threshold=25.0`, `WARMUP_BARS=40`) combining `ml4t-engineer` (`market_regime_classifier`, `roc`, `rsi`, `natr`), `ruptures` PELT RBF changepoints, `river` `ADWIN(delta=0.01)` streaming drift, and `stumpy` `stumpi` matrix-profile anomalies, gated by causal 5-minute RTH reversal triggers (`step_rth_5m_bar`: `orl_brk`, `red`, `vw_fail` for tops; `orh_brk`, `grn`, `vw_rec` for bottoms).
- **Fail-Closed Short Borrow & Inverse Hedge Routing (`live/paper_farm.py` & `tv/tickets.py`):** Post-selection `lookup_alpaca_paper_borrow` checks `shortable` and `AssetBorrowStatus.EASY_TO_BORROW` (`borrow_source="alpaca_paper_asset"`, `short_fee_bps=0.0` ETB vs `30.0` unverified). In `paper_farm.py`, `select_short_or_inverse_fallback` routes `DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` only when verified ETB and `session != "OVERNIGHT"`, failing closed to `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS` / `INVERSE_HEDGE_SQQQ`. In `tv/tickets.py`, `filter_extreme_movers` drops `LOW_MOVE_NOT_EXTREME` / `LOW_RVOL_NOT_EXTREME` (preserving `SOXS`/`SQQQ` crash hedges when `regime == -1`), assigns `READY_HALF_AUTO_HEDGE` or `USE_INVERSE_ETF_OR_VERIFY_BORROW` fail-closed, validates every ticket via `TvAlertV1` (Pydantic v2), and enforces `live_execution_enabled = False`, `semi_auto_confirmed = False`, and `qty = 1`.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Concentrates scarce `200 rpm` Alpaca and `3,000 rpm` IBKR API budgets on high-RVOL extreme movers while aligning direction with causal multi-feed regime and fail-closed ETB/inverse routing.
- **Negative:** Unverified or hard-to-borrow shorts automatically fall back to inverse ETFs (`SOXS`/`SQQQ`), which carry daily leverage reset drag instead of single-name exposure.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Raw HTTP polling (`requests`/`httpx`/`aiohttp`) and unofficial retail screeners violated upstream API contracts, exhausted `200 rpm` rate limits on flat mega-caps, and allowed unverified short attempts. All live scanning, causal regime detection, and borrow routing now flow strictly through typed `alpaca-py` clients, pinned L2 discovery libraries, and fail-closed `TvAlertV1` / `CrossLaneSymbolLock` contracts.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-raw-http-broker-polling`, `no-raw-http-socket-in-src`, `no-banned-library-import`, `no-live-broker-endpoint`, `no-hardcoded-live-execution-true`), `resolve_alpaca_env_creds` (`AK*` live key refusal), `tests/test_live_automation.py` (`test_scanner_filters_flat_megacaps_and_enriches_trf_and_borrow`, `test_finra_trf_ratio_aggregation`, `test_paper_farm_non_overlapping_lanes_and_fail_closed_gates`), `tests/test_tv_automation.py` (`test_tickets_strict_extreme_filter_rungs_and_short_routing`), and `tests/test_core_engine.py` (`test_causal_pit_regime_and_upstream_signals`).
