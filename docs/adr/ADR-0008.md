# ADR-0008: Extreme-Mover Early Entry, Causal Regime Alignment & Market-Aligned Shorting

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy focuses capital and broker API rate budgets strictly on the highest-potential extreme movers early while aligning long/short direction with causal market regime (shorting market crashes and verified reversal peaks). Spending scarce API budget (`200 rpm` Alpaca Paper / `3,000 rpm` IBKR) on flat, low-RVOL mega-caps or fighting broad selloffs degrades compounding expectancy.

## Decision Drivers

- Align long/short direction with causal multi-feed market regime (`SPY`, `QQQ`, `SOXX`, `RSP`, `VIX`): short market crashes (`regime == -1`) and enter extreme movers early via upstream feature/drift/changepoint libraries.
- Short peaks only after E2E-verified causal reversal confirmation (`discovery.py` + `pine/tier0_omni_sota_24x5.pine`).
- Enforce fail-closed short borrow verification (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` vs inverse ETF hedges `SOXS`/`SQQQ`).

## Considered Options

1. **Static large-cap watchlists with unconstrained long/short entries** — wastes RPM on low-movement symbols and risks unborrowable shorts.
2. **Whole-market extreme-mover scanner (`alpaca-py` SIP + `FINRA TRF`) + upstream causal regime/reversal discovery + post-selection ETB lookup + inverse ETF fallback**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 across `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/scanner.py`, and `src/tier0_sota/tv/tickets.py`. Flat/low-RVOL names (`LOW_MOVE_NOT_EXTREME`, `LOW_RVOL_NOT_EXTREME`) are excluded strictly so capital and RPM focus on extreme movers (default pins `SOXL,SOXS` only). Short candidates require verified `shortable && easy_to_borrow` (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB`) via post-selection lookup; unverified or hard-to-borrow symbols route fail-closed to inverse ETF hedges (`AMEX:SOXS` / `NASDAQ:SQQQ`).

### Upstream Evidence & Official Directives

- **Discovery & Market Feeds:** `alpacahq/alpaca-py` (`0.45.0` Screener, SIP bars/trades, FINRA TRF exchange `D`, Paper asset borrow metadata), `pola-rs/polars`, `ml4t/engineer`, `online-ml/river`, `deepcharles/ruptures`, `stumpy-dev/stumpy`, plus vendored `tradermonty/claude-trading-skills` (`stockbee-episodic-pivot-analyzer`, `stockbee-momentum-burst-screener`, `parabolic-short-trade-planner`, `market-top-detector`, `macro-regime-detector`).

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Raw HTTP polling (`requests`/`httpx`/`aiohttp`) and unofficial screeners violated upstream API contracts and rate limits. All scanning and regime analysis now flow exclusively through typed `alpaca-py` clients and pinned L2 discovery libraries.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-raw-http-broker-polling`, `no-raw-http-socket-in-src`), `tests/test_live_automation.py` (`test_scanner_filters_flat_megacaps_and_enriches_trf_and_borrow`, `test_finra_trf_ratio_aggregation`), and `tests/test_tv_automation.py`.

### Compounding Layer Integration

- Emits deterministic `live_whole_market_movers_manifest.json` (`Z:\sota-data\manifests`) feeding both `paper` (3-lane `PaperFarmCoordinator` dispatch) and `half-auto-tv` (`tv/tickets.py`), while historical regime/reversal signals feed `sim-backtest` (`MLflow`) for E2E compounding optimization.
