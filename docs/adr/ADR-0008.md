# ADR-0008: Market-Aligned Always-Short & Pure Extreme-Mover RPM Allocation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Fighting broad market selloffs with blind long entries or wasting precious broker API rate budget (`200 rpm` on Alpaca Paper) on flat, low-RVOL mega-caps destroys expected return per API call.

## Decision Drivers

- Causal multi-feed market regime alignment (`SPY`, `QQQ`, `SOXX`, `RSP`, `VIX`): short market crashes (`regime == -1`) and enter extreme movers early.
- Short peaks only after causal peak-reversal confirmation (`discovery.py` + `pine/tier0_omni_sota_24x5.pine`).
- Fail-closed short borrow enforcement (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` vs `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS_SQQQ`).

## Considered Options

1. **Static large-cap watchlists with unconstrained long/short orders** — wastes RPM on sleepy symbols and risks unborrowable shorts.
2. **Whole-market extreme-mover scanner (`Alpaca SIP` + `FINRA TRF`) + post-selection ETB lookup + inverse ETF fallback**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 across `discovery.py`, `live/scanner.py`, and `tv/tickets.py`. Flat/low-RVOL names (`LOW_MOVE_NOT_EXTREME`, `LOW_RVOL_NOT_EXTREME`) are excluded strictly so capital and RPM focus on extreme movers (default pins `SOXL,SOXS` only). Short candidates verify `shortable && easy_to_borrow` (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB`) via post-selection lookup; unverified or hard-to-borrow names route fail-closed to inverse ETF hedges (`AMEX:SOXS` / `NASDAQ:SQQQ`, `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS_SQQQ`).

### Consequences

- **Positive:** Maximizes capital velocity and rate-budget efficiency while staying aligned with macro trend and borrow rules.
- **Negative:** Requires live `Alpaca SIP`/`FINRA TRF` mover feeds or manifest fixtures to populate non-hedge tickets.
