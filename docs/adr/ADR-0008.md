# ADR-0008: Extreme-Mover Early Entry, Causal Regime Alignment & Market-Aligned Shorting

> [!NOTE]
> **Superseded by [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) and [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md):** To eliminate 3-way scope duplication, this historical record has been split cleanly into [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) (whole-market 24/5 extreme-mover scanning, PIT catalyst filtering, cross-sectional potential ranking, and post-selection ETB/inverse routing) and [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md) (multi-regime strategy sleeves, causal `SPY`/`QQQ`/`SOXX`/`RSP`/`VIX` online regime detection, climax-peak reversal shorts, and `skfolio` portfolio construction).

## Context and Problem Statement

Core Trading Philosophy (`PHILOSOPHY_COMPOUNDING_MANIFEST.json`) focuses capital and broker API rate budgets strictly on the **highest-potential extreme movers early** while aligning long/short direction with causal multi-feed market regime (shorting market crashes where `regime == -1` and verified reversal peaks). Spending scarce API budget (`200 rpm` per Alpaca Paper lane / `3,000 rpm` on IBKR Paper `IBKR_DU_4002`, each reserving `>= 10%` capacity strictly for protective exits) on flat, low-RVOL mega-caps or fighting broad market selloffs degrades compounding expectancy and exposes accounts to unborrowable short rejections.

## Decision Drivers

- **Focus Strictly on Extreme Movers Early:** Reject flat/low-RVOL symbols (`LOW_MOVE_NOT_EXTREME`, `LOW_RVOL_NOT_EXTREME`) unless pinned (`SOXL`, `SOXS`) or acting as crash inverse hedges (`SOXS`, `SQQQ`) when `regime == -1` (`|session_move_pct| >= 3.5%`, `rvol_normalized >= 1.5x`, `price >= $1.50`, `dollar_vol >= $5,000,000`).
- **Align Direction with Causal Multi-Feed Market Regime:** Compute causal `SPY`/`QQQ`/`SOXX`/`RSP`/`VIX` regime (`+1`, `-1`, `0`) strictly on closed bars (`t_close_ns = t_ns + 60s`, `XNYS`).
- **Short Reversal Peaks Only After Causal Confirmation:** Require E2E-verified causal reversal confirmation (`step_rth_5m_bar` RTH 5-minute `ORL5` break, first-red 5m, or `VWAP` fail in `discovery.py`, mirrored in `pine/tier0_omni_sota_24x5.pine` with `barstate.isconfirmed`).
- **Enforce Fail-Closed Short Borrow Verification:** Verify `shortable && easy_to_borrow` (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB`) post-selection via `alpaca_paper_asset`, block overnight direct shorts on Alpaca, route unverified/HTB shorts to inverse ETF hedges (`SOXS`/`SQQQ`), and keep `live_execution_enabled = False`, `paper_only = True`, and `semi_auto_confirmed = False`.

## Considered Options

1. **Static large-cap watchlists with unconstrained long/short entries** — wastes RPM on low-movement symbols, fights broad crash selloffs, lacks peak-reversal confirmation, and risks HTB/unborrowable short rejections.
2. **Whole-market extreme-mover scanner (`alpaca-py` SIP + `FINRA TRF`) + upstream causal regime/reversal discovery + post-selection ETB lookup + fail-closed inverse ETF fallback**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2 across `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/scanner.py`, `src/tier0_sota/live/paper_farm.py`, and `src/tier0_sota/tv/tickets.py`, now split cleanly across [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) (scanning, catalysts, ranking, borrow dispatch) and [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md) (multi-regime sleeves, online regime ensemble, climax-peak reversal shorts, and portfolio construction).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Concentrates scarce `200 rpm` Alpaca and `3,000 rpm` IBKR API budgets on high-RVOL extreme movers while aligning direction with causal multi-feed regime and fail-closed ETB/inverse routing.
- **Negative:** Unverified or hard-to-borrow shorts automatically fall back to inverse ETFs (`SOXS`/`SQQQ`), carrying daily leverage reset drag; keeping scanning and multi-regime strategy combined in one ADR overlapped with [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) and [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md), resolved by formal supersession.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Static large-cap watchlists + unconstrained long/short entries

- Good, because symbol lists never change.
- Bad, because RPM is wasted on flat tickers, market crashes whipsaw longs, and HTB shorts fail at the broker.

### Option 2: Extreme-mover SIP/TRF scanner + causal regime/reversal discovery + fail-closed ETB fallback

- Good, because capital and API quotas concentrate on verified movers aligned with causal market regime and safe short routing.
- Bad, because inverse ETF fallbacks incur leverage-reset drag and require multi-stage manifest coordination.
