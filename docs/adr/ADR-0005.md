# ADR-0005: Formal Immutable Risk Kernel & Z3 SMT Proofs (`risk.py` Rule-612 `<= 2.00%`, `-1.50%` Fuse, `>= 10%` Reserve)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Evidence Grade:** E4

## Context and Problem Statement

Floating-point rounding drift, unquantized sub-dollar ticks, unchecked intraday drawdowns, or broker API rate exhaustion can breach maximum per-trade loss bounds or block protective exits during volatility spikes. Example-based unit tests alone cannot prove universal invariants over all positive prices.

## Decision Drivers

- Exact `Decimal` and integer-tick arithmetic (`float()` forbidden) complying with Reg-NMS Rule-612 (`$0.01 >= $1.00`, `$0.0001 < $1.00`).
- Conservative inward stop quantization guaranteeing initial stop distance never exceeds `2.00%` (`MAX_STOP_LOSS_PCT`).
- Session-latched `-1.50%` daily drawdown circuit breaker and `>= 10%` protective exit API rate reserve.
- Universal machine-checked SMT counterexample refutation via `z3-solver` (`UNSAT`).

## Considered Options

1. **Inline float stop calculations guarded only by example tests** — vulnerable to sub-dollar rounding beyond `2.00%` and untested edge cases.
2. **SHA-256-immutable `src/tier0_sota/risk.py`** formally verified by `evals/formal_risk_z3.py` (`z3-solver` `UNSAT`), `Hypothesis` property fuzzing, and `ast-grep`.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt Option 2. `src/tier0_sota/risk.py` remains byte-identical (SHA-256 `ad8071dcd4196c4c529c02107114ebfdda6692bc9d7c220031f1b19ea3e4d847`, excluded from `ruff format`, protected in `.github/CODEOWNERS`). Every execution, Paper Farm, and TradingView module delegates `quantize_rule_612`, `compute_capped_stop_price`, `size_position_by_risk`, `DailyDrawdownBreaker`, and `ProtectiveExitRateBudget` to `risk.py`.

### Upstream Evidence & Official Directives

- **Formal Verification & Property Testing:** `Z3Prover/z3` (`z3-solver` in `evals/formal_risk_z3.py`), `HypothesisWorks/hypothesis` (`tests/test_core_engine.py`), and SEC Reg-NMS Rule 612 minimum pricing increments.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson Learned:** Tracking arbitrary line-count caps on `risk.py` or formatting it with automated formatters risked silent hash drift without adding mathematical safety. Immutability is enforced strictly by cryptographic SHA-256 + formal SMT proof, never LOC caps.
- **Mechanical Upstream Gates:** `evals/formal_risk_z3.py` (`Z3_FORMAL_RISK_PROOFS_UNSAT_OK=1`), `rules/zero_self_invention.yml` (`no-float-cast-in-risk`), `scripts/sync_manifest.py --check`, `tests/test_architecture_manifest.py` (`test_risk_kernel_sha256_exact`), `tests/test_formal_and_evals.py`, and `Dagster` asset `risk_kernel_sha256_attestation`.

### Compounding Layer Integration

- Underpins all 4 compounding lanes (`sim-backtest`, `paper`, `half-auto-tv`, `live`): every backtest stop, Paper Farm risk size, TV ticket rung, and Stage-5 live attestation carries cryptographic `risk.py` SHA-256 + `Z3` UNSAT provenance in `Dagster`, `MLflow`, and `ARCHITECTURE_MANIFEST.json`.
