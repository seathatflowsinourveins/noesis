# ADR-0013: Multi-Tier Market, Microstructure, Borrow & Fundamental Data Feeds Convergence

## Context and Problem Statement

The Core Trading Philosophy needs four kinds of data, each with its own latency, licensing, and point-in-time (PIT) requirements:

1. **Market tape (L1/bars):** finding extreme movers across the whole market and classifying the causal regime (`SPY`, `QQQ`, `SOXX`, `RSP`, `VIX`).
2. **Microstructure (L2 `MBP-10` / L3 `MBO`):** queue-position markout and fill realism for `hftbacktest` and `NautilusTrader v2` parity ([ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)).
3. **Borrow / short-sale state:** ETB/HTB routing (`DIRECT_SHORT_ETB` vs `INVERSE_HEDGE_SOXS_SQQQ`), Reg SHO short volume, FINRA short interest.
4. **Fundamentals & macro:** SEC 8-K/10-Q/Form-4 events with `accepted_ns <= as_of_ns`, share count and float, and ALFRED/FRED vintages.

Market structure changes on **2026-12-06**: from Sunday 21:00 ET, the CTA/UTP SIPs run **23x5** (Sun 21:00 ET to Fri 20:00 ET, with a daily 20:00 to 21:00 ET maintenance pause), while broker overnight venues (Blue Ocean ATS via Alpaca `DataFeed.BOATS` / `DataFeed.OVERNIGHT`, and IBKR `OVERNIGHT`) start at 20:00 ET. Without one tiered feed policy, the data plane risks unvalidated scrapers, mixed timestamp semantics, post-wall leakage, or non-OSI data infrastructure.

## Decision Drivers

- **Upstream-only feeds:** every feed must come from an official SDK or adapter pinned in `uv.lock`, or from a public regulator dataset (SEC EDGAR, FINRA, ALFRED/FRED). Unofficial scrapers (`yfinance`, `pandas-datareader`) are banned.
- **One PIT contract across all tiers ([ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)):** pre-wall cutoff `<= 2026-10-15` (`DISCOVERY_WALL_UTC_NS`, `PreWallViolationError`), bar availability `t_close_ns = t_ns + 60s`, SEC `accepted_ns <= as_of_ns`, FINRA T-1 / publication-date alignment, ALFRED `vintage_date <= as_of`.
- **24/5 -> 23x5 continuity:** session labels must match the SIP 23x5 timetable and broker overnight (BOATS) timetable after 2026-12-06. `OVERNIGHT` direct shorts stay blocked (`paper_farm.submit_guarded_entry` refuses `SHORT` + `OVERNIGHT`).
- **Borrow truth before shorting:** verify `shortable`, `easy_to_borrow`, and `borrow_status` post-selection using the official `alpaca-py` asset API (`live/scanner.py`), with IBKR paper (`:4002`) generic tick `236` as a second opinion only.
- **SPDX OSI license firewall:** client libraries and storage formats must use OSI-approved licenses (`MIT`, `Apache-2.0`, `BSD`, `LGPL-3.0-only`).
- **Safety invariants unchanged:** `paper_only=True`, `live_execution_enabled=False`, immutable `src/tier0_sota/risk.py`, and fail-closed live broker ports `4001/7496/8798/8796`.

## Considered Options

1. **Single retail feed (IEX-only bars) plus web scraping for borrow and fundamentals** — IEX misses consolidated liquidity, and scraped borrow/filings lack PIT provenance and violate the banned-library gate.
2. **One commercial all-in-one vendor for L3 + fundamentals + borrow** — concentrates vendor lock-in, loses regulator-sourced PIT provenance, and duplicates native `NautilusTrader` adapters.
3. **Multi-tier convergence on official upstream SDKs and regulator datasets, with earned-trigger promotion for Tier B L2/L3 depth and `L11` lake tooling (chosen)**.

## Decision Outcome

Adopt **Option 3**. Every feed enters through existing upstream adapters with zero custom wrappers:

- **Tier A — Market Tape (Active):** `alpaca-py==0.45.0` (`DataFeed.SIP` whole-market scan + FINRA TRF `Exchange.D` darkpool share; `DataFeed.BOATS` / `DataFeed.OVERNIGHT` for 20:00–04:00 ET indicative overnight quotes; historical 1-minute SIP bars in `Z:\sota-data\prewall-lake` per [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)).
- **Tier B — Microstructure (Earned Trigger):** Databento `MBP-10` (`OrderBookDepth10`) and `MBO` (`OrderBookDelta`), consumed strictly through the **native NautilusTrader Databento adapter** (`nautilus_trader.adapters.databento`, `DatabentoDataLoader` -> `Z:\sota-data\nautilus-catalog`) and `databento-python` (`Apache-2.0`). Promoted only when an `hftbacktest` markout gate needs L2/L3 depth beyond SIP L1 (`uv.lock` has `0` `databento` entries today).
- **Tier C — Borrow / Short-Sale (Active):** Alpaca asset API (`shortable`, `easy_to_borrow`, `borrow_status`, `overnight_tradable`), FINRA Reg SHO daily short volume, and FINRA Rule 4560 twice-monthly short interest (joined strictly on publication date — 7th business day after settlement — never on settlement date). IBKR paper generic tick `236` -> `tickType 46` (`<= 1.5` not shortable, `1.5..2.5` HTB, `> 2.5` ETB) as second opinion only via `:4002`.
- **Tier D — Fundamentals & Macro (Active):** `edgartools==6.4.0` (`MIT`, keyed on EDGAR `accepted` timestamps) and `fredapi==0.5.2` (`Apache-2.0`, ALFRED vintages wired offline-first via `discovery.load_causal_fred_vintages` in `src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`).
- **Bulk-History Watch:** Massive (formerly Polygon.io) S3 flat files remain watch-only (commercial data-licensing decision; OSI client required if triggered).
- **`L11` Lake Candidates (`SOTA_STARRED_AND_BEYOND_MANIFEST.json`, watch-only until triggers fire):**

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Prevents vendor-scraper and timestamp-mixing bugs by routing all tape, microstructure, borrow, and SEC/ALFRED tiers through pinned OSI SDKs and one PIT contract ([ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)), ready for the 2026-12-06 SIP 23x5 cutover.
- **Negative:** Defers L2/L3 depth (`databento`) and `L11` lake upgrades (`vortex`, `lancedb`, `sqlglot`, `dlt`) until quantified triggers fire, limiting pre-promotion queue calibration to L1 SIP bars.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Single retail IEX feed + web scraping

- Good, because subscription cost is minimal.
- Bad, because IEX misses consolidated volume and scraped fundamentals/borrow destroy PIT provenance.

### Option 2: Single commercial all-in-one vendor

- Good, because all schemas come from one vendor API.
- Bad, because it creates lock-in, weakens regulator-origin PIT auditability, and duplicates native `NautilusTrader` adapters.

### Option 3: Multi-tier convergence on official OSI SDKs + regulator datasets + earned-trigger `L11`/Tier B

- Good, because every tier uses authoritative timestamps and OSI-verified adapters while avoiding speculative infrastructure bloat.
- Bad, because promoting Tier B L2/L3 depth requires a measured `hftbacktest` markout trigger and explicit ADR amendment.

### Self-Invention Lesson Learned & Mechanical Upstream Gate

- **Lesson learned:** Hand-rolling Databento DBN decoders, REST borrow pollers, or short-interest scrapers duplicates adapters already provided by `nautilus_trader`, `alpaca-py`, and regulator files while obscuring settlement vs publication dates. Every tier now carries its upstream's native timestamp and passes through one PIT layer (`src/tier0_sota/discovery.py`, [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md)).
- **Mechanical gates:** `upstream-map.toml` `banned_libraries`/`banned_packages` + `spdx_license_firewall`, `rules/zero_self_invention.yml`, `deptry`, `PreWallViolationError`, `paper_farm` `PermissionError` on non-paper modes, `forbidden_live_hosts = ["api.alpaca.markets"]`, `forbidden_live_ports`, and `just check`.

### Compounding Layer Integration

- **`sim-backtest`:** Pre-wall SIP bars + SEC/FINRA/ALFRED tables feed `MLflow` trials and `Dagster` asset `prewall_pit_audit`; Tier B depth lands in `Z:\sota-data\nautilus-catalog` for `hftbacktest` markout ([ADR-0016](ADR-0016.md)) once promoted.
- **`paper` & `half-auto-tv`:** Tier A SIP/BOATS + Tier C borrow checks drive the 4-account Paper Farm ([ADR-0009](ADR-0009.md)) and `TvAlertV1` tickets ([ADR-0010](ADR-0010.md)).
- **`live`:** Consumes identical feed contracts while remaining `live_execution_enabled = False` / `paper_only = True` under the Stage-1-to-Stage-5 promotion protocol governed by [ADR-0020](ADR-0020.md).

### Living Evolution & Supersession Policy

- Living MADR 4.0 snapshot governed by [ADR-0024](ADR-0024.md): amend in place or supersede when an `L11` trigger fires, Tier B depth graduates after measured `hftbacktest` lift, or the 2026-12-06 SIP 23x5 cutover updates feed enums, always preserving `risk.py`, `paper_only=True`, and live-port isolation.
